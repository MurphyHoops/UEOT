## 7. Boundaries

### Same micro model is explicit

The semantic theorem compares two quotient descriptions of one literal micro
control model.  Encoder gauge convergence alone cannot identify two different
underlying physical/control systems.

### Exact quotients are required

The present theorem does not derive exact quotient structure from approximate
reward/transition defects.  That job belongs to the prior approximate-history
encoder and exact-closure lanes.

### Equal quotient carrier cardinality

The weighted gauge lane currently compares encoders into the same finite
carrier type `S`.  Cross-cardinality model selection is not included.

### Greedy tie-breaking is not gauge-invariant

Only Bellman-optimality of the transported action is claimed.  Literal equality
of canonical greedy selectors would be false without a unique-argmax or fixed
tie-breaking compatibility assumption.

### GOA law transport is not yet included

The macro transition kernel is transported exactly, so a subsequent theorem can
transport closed-loop invariant laws under the corresponding selector.  This
lane stops before introducing that additional probability-simplex relabeling.
