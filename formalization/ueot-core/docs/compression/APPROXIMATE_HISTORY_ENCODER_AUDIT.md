This lane strengthens the finite approximate Agency -> GOD -> GOA program by
moving the approximation assumptions one layer earlier.

PR #180 starts from an already constructed `ApproxControlQuotient` and then
proves near-optimal control plus time-varying near-GOA tracking.  The present

- one finite micro/history control model;

It constructs the macro model and the approximate quotient from those data.

## 1. Representation defect

For histories `h,h'` in the same encoder fibre, assume uniformly for every
action `a`

\[
|r(h,a)-r(h',a)|\le \varepsilon_r,
\]

and

\[
D_{TV}
\bigl(C_\#P(\cdot\mid h,a),
      C_\#P(\cdot\mid h',a)\bigr)
\le \varepsilon_p.
\]

These assumptions are direct quantitative relaxations of exact fibre
compatibility: histories identified by the encoder need not be identical for
control, but their immediate reward and encoded next-state law must remain
uniformly close.

## 2. Canonical representative macro model

Surjectivity of `C` allows one representative history to be chosen for every
encoded state.

`representativeMacroModel` defines the macro reward and transition from that
representative:

- macro reward = representative micro reward;
- macro transition = pushforward of the representative micro transition under
  `C`.

The model's stochasticity and reward bound are inherited directly from the
micro model.

The representative choice is not claimed to be canonical in a semantic sense.
Its role is only to provide one concrete witness model.  The within-fibre
diameter assumptions certify that every other history in the same fibre is
within the same error radii of that witness.

## 3. Approximate quotient generation

`approximateQuotientOfFiberDefects` proves that the representative macro model
and the original micro model form an `ApproxControlQuotient` with exactly the
supplied radii `εr` and `εp`.

There is no independently postulated macro approximation premise.

The reward approximation follows directly from the within-fibre reward bound.
The transition approximation follows from the within-fibre TV bound and the
identity

`representativeMacroModel_transitionPMF`.

Thus the approximation certificate is generated by the encoder geometry in
control-relevant coordinates.

## 7. Relation to M-QD

The exact M-QD principle says that exact fibre compatibility permits exact
descent through a quotient representation.

The present theorem is a quantitative analogue at the control interface, but
it is **not** promoted as a new counted M-QD theorem.  Its proof uses the
existing approximate-control quotient machinery plus a representative witness.

The scientific relation is:

- zero fibre defect is the exact-descending limit;

A future zero-defect closure theorem may connect this module more directly to
the exact structural-defect / M-QD lane.

## 8. Boundaries

### Finite state and action

The theorem is finite-state and uses one uniform finite action type.  It does
not yet descend state-dependent admissible-action fibres.

### Surjective encoder

Every macro state must be represented by at least one micro/history state.  An
ambient macro space with unreachable coordinates must first be restricted to
the reachable image.

### One-step control sufficiency, not full path-law equivalence

The fibre assumptions control reward and encoded one-step transition laws.
They do not separately prove equality or closeness of an independently given
history-space path law to the generated Markov path law.

### Encoder is supplied, not learned

The theorem certifies a candidate representation.  It does not discover `C`
from data or prove statistical generalization of estimated fibre defects.

### Baseline policy near-optimality is not per-time drifting optimality

The `2D` guarantee refers to the baseline micro model and the policy lifted
from its constructed macro quotient.  The time-varying models `Mseq n` are used
only for long-run state-law tracking under that same policy.  No claim is made
that this policy is optimal or `2D`-optimal for every drifting model.

### Dobrushin remains sufficient, not necessary

Strict `alpha < 1` is used for a unique globally attracting reference law.  It
does not characterize every possible GOA or recurrent long-run structure.
