## 7. Repair-mechanism deletion boundary

The present finite Omega-loop certificate repairs **state/controller damage
under an intact repair law**.

That distinction is formally visible rather than merely philosophical:

- `controllerRepairLift` and `autonomousRepairLift` are transition laws passed
  to the path construction; they are not coordinates stored inside
  `ConstitutiveState`;
- `PhysicalRepairCertificate` is supplied to O5 as constitutive theorem data;
- O1 already proves that if one instead runs the old persistence-only
  `constitutiveLift`, a non-preserving controller remains non-preserving at all
  finite marginals.

Therefore Track O has **not** shown that the repair rule itself can be damaged,
reconstructed, copied, or selected from internal state.  Arbitrary deletion of
the repair mechanism is outside the O5/O7 basin theorem.

This is a genuine G3 Objecthood boundary and is sufficient evidence for O8;
adding another Lean theorem that merely renames the existing O1 no-repair
witness would be redundant.

## 8. Exact boundary to stronger self-production / autopoiesis

O7/O8 now justify the phrase **finite self-repairing constitutive Omega-loop
certificate** under explicit assumptions.  The following stronger claims
remain open and must not be imported into that phrase:

1. **repair-law self-maintenance** — the mechanism that chooses/executes repair
   is external to the reflexive state and is not itself repaired;
2. **ontogenetic self-construction** — formation is certified by Track X, but
   O7 does not construct the repair architecture from an initially absent
   organization;
3. **resource / energetic closure** — no theorem models material, energetic,
   computational, or thermodynamic cost needed to maintain and rebuild the
   organization;
4. **recurrent/adversarial fault tolerance** — the proved target is transient
   damage in an explicit basin, not indefinite survival under arbitrary or
   strategically repeated faults;
5. **adaptive repair synthesis** — the P-REC policy/certificate is supplied;
   the object does not learn a new repair policy when the old one is invalid;
6. **repair of semantic machinery** — Track-X long-run semantic certificates
   are preserved as specifications, not dynamically reconstructed after their
   own destruction;
7. **structural turnover identity** — functional identity is presently tied to
   one formed parent, its persistence kernel, and a class of preserving
   controllers; arbitrary replacement of the parent-forming architecture is
   not covered;
8. **self-reproduction / lineage** — no copy/reproduction theorem is part of
   Track O;
9. **infinite/continuous generality** — the core O2/O3/O5/O7 construction is a
   finite-state theorem architecture;
10. **universal or biological autopoiesis** — no equivalence to biological
    autopoiesis or universal Objecthood is proved.

The mathematically sharp residual can be summarized as:

```text
proved:
  intact repair law
  + explicit repair basin/certificate
  + transient internal damage
  -> autonomous state/controller recovery to the same certified parent

not proved:
  damaged/absent repair-producing organization
  -> internal reconstruction of the repair law and of the organization that
     generates it.
```

That second arrow is the proper next boundary for any future stronger
autopoiesis track.
