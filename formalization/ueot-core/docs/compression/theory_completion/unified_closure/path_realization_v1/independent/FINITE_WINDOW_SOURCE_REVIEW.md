## Actual finite-window source bridge

The model, initial PMF, observation, stationary current-observation feedback,
and safe set are shared through the chain. The observation type is arbitrary;
state and action types remain finite. Numerical homogeneity belongs to this
specific stationary-source theorem and is not inferred from mere support
homogeneity or from a generic finite-memory policy.

The private weighted suffix calculation starts with the original recursive
state-history PMF, expands its actual `pmfExtend` transition, and sums safe
continuations. It therefore establishes a source equality rather than assuming
that a matrix formula is already the actual survival experiment. The constant
terminal test yields the joint event identity

\[
\Pr(\text{state prefix}=h,\ \text{safe through }t+r)
=\Pr(\text{state prefix}=h)\,(Q^r\mathbf 1)(\operatorname{last}(h))
\]

for each safe prefix `h`. Positivity is not required for this identity. The
zero-step, time-zero, zero-prefix-mass, and zero-survival cases are retained.

`actualConditionedPrefix` filters the original complete action/state experiment
on its entire survival event and only then erases actions and drops the suffix.
Its support theorem derives safety of the retained prefix from `PMF.filter`
and the actual map. Its unsafe-atom theorem follows from that support result.
The atom formula supplies both the future-survival tilt of the original initial
law and the complete safe-path weight. The initial safe row is the restriction
of the original initial PMF and is not normalized in advance.

The next-row theorem compares actual child and parent joint events with the
same final time, `(t + 1) + r = t + (r + 1)`. Positive **parent event mass**
implies a positive original prefix atom and a positive remaining-survival
denominator. Positive total survival alone is not used as a substitute. The
resulting ratio is

\[
Q(i,j)\frac{(Q^r\mathbf 1)(j)}{(Q^{r+1}\mathbf 1)(i)}.
\]

`finiteWindowRowPMF` separately proves that this is a PMF on a row with positive
remaining survival. The source/API claim is the actual conditioned-prefix
formula and actual child/parent ratio together with this positive-row PMF.
The module does **not** construct an independent total time-dependent
conditional kernel on all states, choose null-row fallbacks, or prove a new
full-path law generated by such a total kernel. The source contract states
this boundary accurately.

## Fixed Doob path comparison

The Doob rows are constructed from the existing positive right eigenstructure
of the same matrix. The initial law is the natural normalized right-vector
tilt of the original safe row, with explicit pairing `c = ⟨η₀,h⟩ > 0`.

The actual finite Doob PMF recursion yields, by cancellation of interior
right-vector factors,

\[
\Pr_D(g)
=\frac{\operatorname{killedPathWeight}(g)h(g_T)}{\rho^T c}.
\]

The source comparison combines this with the previously proved actual
conditioned-path atom. On a positive killed path, equality of the two atoms
is equivalent to `h(g_T) = ρ^T c / s_T`. This condition is restricted to actual
positive paths; it does not demand equality on unreachable states.

The separate condition `Q 1 = ρ 1`, with positive `ρ`, correctly gives
`Q^r 1 = ρ^r 1` and the row formula `Q/ρ`. It is a sufficient condition, not a
consequence asserted from a left QSD. No spectral convergence, Q-process limit,
or asymptotic path identification is claimed.
