## 1. Central problem and scientific north star

**Given one explicitly shared world process, action/probe family, time ordering and observation model, construct — rather than independently stipulate — the strongest possible compatible predictive, dynamic, object-identity, causal provenance, teleological and lifetime structures, and prove which additional premises are necessary.**

Candidate common mathematical spine:
`Process + protocols → minimal predictive representation → admissible continuation/formation → causal identity/provenance [extra information] → control/viability → conditional construction/lineage`.

Every arrow must be a theorem, conditional theorem, typed adapter, no-go, conjecture or empirical hypothesis. No arrow may be asserted simply because an identical word such as Object, Source, GOA, or RG appears in two modules. Do not assume a universal source-free teleology, no-go-defying ancestral identity, spontaneous full autopoiesis, or unconditional universal Π/Φ mechanism.

**Deeper mathematical conjecture:** a category/diagram of *typed process representations equipped with transferable certificates* may supply a genuine cross-lane organizational calculus. To count as a mathematical improvement it must discharge an existing independent premise or prove a new quantitative/cross-layer consequence, not merely package already supplied hypotheses.

## 4. Universal nine-gate checklist

1. **G0 — Reuse/provenance:** exact canonical paths and Lean declaration names; dependency graph acyclic.
2. **G1 — Same-source typing:** joint bottom process, time, action and observation model genuinely shared (or an independently proved transport), not a product of unrelated theorem witnesses.
3. **G2 — Correct implication:** Lean kernel checks conclusion under weakest declared hypotheses; stronger claims are separately classified.
4. **G3 — Joint nonvacuity:** a completely specified, nontrivial same-process model satisfies all joint premises; empty/vacuous examples flagged.
5. **G4 — Countermodels:** no-go for any unconditional arrow; preserve ambiguous/uncovered when indistinguishable.
6. **G5 — Statistical scope:** genuine sampling/law match, adaptive/dependent-data validity and confidence budget; `E|Y-r| ≠ |EY-r|` when not deterministic.
7. **G6 — Nontrivial consolidation:** demonstrably discharge one formerly separate premise or yield a new cross-layer theorem, not merely relabel a conjunction.
8. **G7 — Exact-head proof checks:** focused Lean; `lake build UEOT`; selected axiom prints; proof-escape scanner, negative controls, track ownership.
9. **G8 — Claim boundary:** predicted state vs physical object identity vs parent genealogy vs purpose vs metaphysical universality remain typed separately.

Each gate is independently `OPEN`, `LOCAL_PROVED`, `MERGED_UNCOUNTED`, `BOUNDARY_PROVED`, or `BLOCKED`. Stage closure does not imply FULL unified theory.
