# UEOT Theory Completion — Claim Policy v1

Status: **FROZEN CLAIM DISCIPLINE**

## Allowed claim classes

Every Theory Completion result has exactly one primary class:

1. **THEOREM** — proved in Lean from declared assumptions.
2. **CONDITIONAL THEOREM** — proved in Lean with essential additional domain/regularity/identifiability assumptions.
3. **ADAPTER / INTERFACE** — typed bridge between existing theorem families; it creates no stronger science than its inputs.
4. **NO-GO / BOUNDARY THEOREM** — proves that a stronger identification/conclusion fails or needs additional assumptions.
5. **CONJECTURE** — mathematically specified but not proved.
6. **EMPIRICAL HYPOTHESIS / CONTRACT** — validation depends on data, estimators, interventions or hardware/experimental evidence.

Narrative summaries preserve the formal class.

## Prohibited upgrades

Do not promote:
- adapter → theorem of new physical law;
- conditional theorem → unconditional theorem;
- existence → uniqueness;
- equivalence class → unique representative;
- convergence under certificate → generic convergence;
- empirical success → mathematical theorem;
- structural analogy → identity.

## Object claims

Do not identify Object with a carrier alone. An operational object claim exposes the relevant predictive/interface/persistence/recovery/semantic-identity structure. If one component is absent, state the weaker object concept.

Objecthood alone does not imply a unique purpose unless a theorem proves identifiability under explicit assumptions.

## Purpose claims

Keep distinct scalar objective, policy/preference ordering, maximizer equivalence, teleological equivalence class and Π/Φ representation class.

General ordinal representation does not imply positive-affine uniqueness. Π/Φ decomposition is not universal unless a converse representation theorem is proved.

## GOD claims

General GOD is an optimal local action/policy/direction correspondence. A unique gradient-vector GOD may be claimed only where differentiability, geometry and uniqueness assumptions justify it.

## GOA claims

Do not equate GOA universally with a unique fixed point. Declare the actual long-run object: fixed point, invariant law, recurrent structure, occupation/Cesàro limit, QSD, optimal long-run structure, or another explicit specialization.

Uniqueness/stability/convergence require their own certificates.

## Scale claims

Do not call quotient = RG, coarse graining = Wilsonian RG, or resolution transport = physical renormalization without an explicit bridge theorem.

## Resource/autopoiesis claims

Resource accounting is not thermodynamic closure without a justified thermodynamic domain bridge.

Repair-law reconstruction is not full autopoiesis.

P12 may return FULL, CONDITIONAL, or PARTIAL / EXPLICIT BOUNDARY.

## Same-object composition rule

Any P2 terminal theorem carries identity guards sufficient to establish that Objecthood, effective state, teleological contract, control dynamics, GOD, GOA and self-maintenance statements concern the same endogenous object.

Sharing only a type, carrier shape or isomorphic representation is insufficient.

## Counted status

Track-TC results are uncounted by default. Only L3 promotion/re-finalization can change counted generator/minimal-core status.
