## 2. Non-negotiable anti-cosmetic gates

A second-order generator is **not** promoted merely because one generic
structure can be written around the existing theorems.

### S1 — Exact wrapper reduction

At least two existing counted generators must obtain materially thinner
canonical theorem surfaces through the new layer, without strengthening source
assumptions or importing the frozen source endpoint.

### S2 — New theorem generation

The candidate must generate at least one nontrivial theorem that was **not**
among the 106 frozen P-IDs used to infer the pattern.

Initial out-of-sample target:

> approximate quotient representations whose within-fibre defect is uniformly
> bounded by an envelope tending to zero and whose pointwise representations
> converge have an exact, unique quotient representation in the limit.

### S3 — Retained-boundary discipline

The experiment must preserve the distinctions already exposed by first-order
compression:

- set-level quotient descent does not imply measurable/continuous/kernel
  descent;
- a transport certificate does not imply its defect tends to zero;
- asymptotic invariance requires topology/continuity or an equivalent limiting
  interface;
- long-run invariance is not attraction, uniqueness, or optimality;
- M-PE is not assumed to reduce to the candidate.

### S4 — Deletion/nonredundancy audit

If a second-order candidate eventually claims to replace or subsume any
first-order generator, run a registered-theorem-surface deletion audit analogous
to Gate C.  Raw Lean/Mathlib re-proving is not an allowed replacement route.

### S5 — Domain transfer

Before counting, apply the candidate to at least one theorem family or assembly
result that was not used to infer it.

Preferred stress test:
- reconstruct a nontrivial part of P-CORE-01 or another cross-module assembly
  theorem from the first-order compression interfaces.

## 5. Relation to Core v3.0

This mission is based on the actual v3.0 architecture, not earlier UEOT
philosophical drafts.

Core v3.0 already establishes the following conceptual separations:

- objecthood does not uniquely determine a value function;
- purpose enters through an explicit evaluation bridge;
- Π/Φ is a typed resource/information template with gauge non-identifiability,
  not a proved universal two-dimensional law;
- Ω is derived from an operational object certificate and failure mechanism
  structure, not identified with one literal topological loop mechanism;
- GOD and GOA are typed control/long-run interfaces;
- measurable optimal selectors do not automatically make the closed loop
  Feller.

The second-order mission therefore does **not** claim those separations as new.
Its only target is new structure revealed by the completed Lean proof graph.
