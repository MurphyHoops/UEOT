## 2. Promotion criteria used here

A post-Gate-D candidate is not promoted merely because a generic theorem can be
written. The audit asks:

1. **Exactness** — does a wrapper recover the frozen source-facing statement
   without weakening quantifiers, topology, zero-probability semantics, or
   control assumptions?
2. **Cross-family reuse** — does the same generic mechanism materially serve at
   least two distinct P-ID families?
3. **Proof compression** — does the generic layer remove duplicated
   mathematical work rather than only rename a final one-line fact?
4. **Ablation nonredundancy** — after excluding the source endpoint, do the
   remaining counted generators plus pre-existing registered adapters already
   contain a theorem-surface route that reconstructs the mapping without the
   candidate?
5. **Boundary preservation** — are source-specific assumptions kept visible?
6. **No cosmetic standard-math promotion** — a standard lemma may be useful,
   but it is not automatically a UEOT generator unless the reusable interface
   captures a real recurring architectural obligation.

### 3.2 Exactness

**P-PRED-03: PASS.**

The wrapper preserves:

- countable response coordinates;

- measurable joint update;
- recovery from the canonical coordinate state;
- fibre invariance under equal canonical state;
- the source convention that zero-probability observations may use an

**P-REF-02: PARTIAL ONLY.**

The generic Bayes coordinate exactly covers the finite positive-evidence

- Standard-Borel posterior/disintegration for general observations;
- posterior/observation reconstruction of the one-step joint law;
- posterior averaging consistency;
- finite evidence normalization;
- explicit `modelConflict`;

Those are not generated by the finite coordinate ratio alone.

### 3.3 Cross-family reuse

Conceptual reuse across P-PRED and P-REF is genuine:

[

However, the exact source-facing coverage is asymmetric:

- P-PRED-03: full exact mapping;
- P-REF-02: one important finite formula plus boundary, not the full P-ID

Therefore the strict two-exact-family generator gate is **not yet met**.

### 3.5 M-BU verdict

## 4. M-OI-01 — Occupation-Limit Invariance

### 4.1 New Lean surface

Module:
`UEOT/V3/Compression/OccupationLimitInvariance.lean`

The experiment proves a generic residual-limit theorem:

[
F_n	o F_*,quad
G_n	o G_*,quad
F_n-G_n	o0
Longrightarrow
F_*=G_*.
]

It then proves source-facing wrappers for:

- **P-GOA-01** — finite-chain Cesaro cluster points are invariant;
- **P-PER-02** — tight Feller occupation limits are invariant and retain the
  original closed-support clauses.

### 4.2 P-GOA-01 exactness

**PASS.**

The wrapper preserves the full frozen statement:

- existence of a convergent Cesaro subsequence by compactness of the finite
  simplex;
- every convergent subsequential limit is invariant;
- no uniqueness claim;
- no full-Cesaro-convergence claim.

The domain adapter remains explicit:

`cesaro_residual_tendsto_zero`

which derives the vanishing one-step defect from the finite Cesaro telescoping
identity.

M-OI supplies the common limit-identification step.

### 4.3 P-PER-02 exactness

**PASS.**

The wrapper preserves:

- arbitrary diverging horizon sequence;
- tightness of the literal occupation family;
- weakly convergent subsequence extraction;
- whole-semigroup invariance;
- closed-support inheritance;
- the universal statement for every other convergent diverging occupation
  sequence.

The domain-specific adapters remain explicit:

- tightness/Prokhorov extraction;
- Feller continuity through bounded continuous observables;
- the occupation shift bound;
- Portmanteau closed-support inheritance.

### 4.4 Cross-family reuse

**PASS.**

P-PER and P-GOA are distinct frozen P-ID families. Their extraction mechanisms
are different, but the invariant-limit closure is the same architecture:

[
	ext{cluster point}
+
	ext{asymptotically vanishing evolution defect}
+
	ext{continuity/separating observables}
Longrightarrow
	ext{invariant long-run object}.
]

This is exactly the conceptual bridge that motivated the original analysis-only
M-OI candidate.

### 4.5 Proof-compression audit

The result is mixed but real.

For P-GOA-01, the frozen endpoint previously inlined:

- continuity of the residual map;
- transport of subsequence convergence through that residual;
- comparison with the independently vanishing residual;
- uniqueness of the limit.

The M-OI wrapper delegates the final limit-identification calculus to one
generic theorem.

For P-PER-02, the source already contains the named helper
`invariant_of_occupation_tendsto`. The full P-PER-02 endpoint was already
short. M-OI therefore does not substantially shorten that endpoint. Instead it
shows that the *inside* of the existing Feller helper and the inlined finite
GOA argument share a generic residual-limit mechanism.

Accordingly, M-OI is genuine semantic/proof-schema compression, but not a large
raw-LOC compression of P-PER-02.

### 4.6 Ablation

The two mappings behave differently.

**P-PER-02 survives M-OI deletion** if the pre-existing source helper
`invariant_of_occupation_tendsto` is retained as a registered adapter.
Therefore P-PER-02 is not a valid nonredundancy witness.

**P-GOA-01 is the candidate nonredundancy witness.**

Its source endpoint currently inlines the residual-limit argument. Outside the
endpoint itself, the retained source surface supplies:

- finite-simplex compact subsequence extraction;
- `cesaro_residual_tendsto_zero`;
- continuity/linear-algebra side conditions.

It does not contain a separately registered theorem node that converts a
convergent subsequence plus vanishing residual into invariance. Under the same
frozen theorem-surface rule used by Gate C, reconstructing that macro step from
raw Mathlib limit uniqueness would create a new derivation node — precisely the
node now formalized as M-OI.

Therefore M-OI has a plausible Gate-C-style nonredundancy witness through
P-GOA-01.

This is a theorem-surface architectural claim, not absolute logical
independence: the generic residual-limit lemma is standard topology/algebra.

### 4.7 Standard-math / UEOT-specific boundary

The primitive theorem `equal_at_limit_of_residual` is standard mathematics.

What is UEOT-relevant is the recurring architectural decomposition:

1. construct a long-horizon empirical/occupation object;
2. prove precompactness/tightness or otherwise obtain a cluster point;
3. prove an evolution defect vanishes;
4. pass the evolution relation through the limit;
5. interpret the resulting invariant object as persistence/GOA structure.

The promotion question is therefore architectural, not a novelty claim about
the residual-limit lemma itself.

### 4.8 Third-family search

The following nearby families were audited as potential extension targets:

- **P-PER-01:** shares continuity/limit/shift reasoning, but its exact
  omega-limit *set* invariance — especially reverse inclusion for a one-sided
  semiflow — needs a predecessor subsequence construction. It is not generated
  by the present M-OI theorem.
- **P-QSD-01:** shares a limit-to-invariance flavor, but the conclusion is
  projective/eigenmeasure invariance after normalization. Survival
  multiplicativity and the exponential law are additional structure. It should
  not be forced into M-OI without a separate projective-limit theorem.
- **P-GOA-02..04:** use Dobrushin contraction, recurrent decomposition, or
  symmetric spectral perturbation. They are long-run stability theorems but not
  instances of occupation-limit invariance.

No third exact P-ID mapping is claimed.

### 4.9 M-OI verdict

**PROMOTION CANDIDATE — REQUIRES INDEPENDENT REVIEW BEFORE LEDGER CHANGE.**

M-OI now passes:

- full build and compression guard;
- two exact source-facing mappings;

It does *not* establish:

- a novel mathematical theorem;
- a universal long-run generator;
- P-PER-01, P-QSD-01, or P-GOA-02..04 generation;
- automatic replacement of the frozen Gate-D core.

The strongest honest next action is an independent promotion audit of the
current branch, followed — only if CLEAR — by a dedicated post-Gate-D
