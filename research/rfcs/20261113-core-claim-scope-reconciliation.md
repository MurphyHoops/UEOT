## 1. Scope and Problem

The current mathematical and philosophical books and successor contracts
already distinguish conditional mechanisms, object roles, counterexamples,
interpretations, and empirical limits. The earlier constitution and canonical
claims still impose universal internal feedback, coupled optimization,
mandatory GOD/GOA, and three-phase lifecycle language. Their method template
makes those requirements operational for every domain memo.

The required change is an explicit reconciliation of protected meanings and
admission rules. It does not repair a demonstrated contradiction in the Lean
theory. Organizational self-regeneration and an operational minimal-failure
family are different predicates. Two qualitative concerns and a common smooth
two-dimensional representation are different claims. Their respective
counterexamples do not establish a contradiction between every possible old
philosophical interpretation and current mathematics.

The unchanged architecture keeps `constitution/` as governance root, `core/`
as domain-neutral mathematical source, and realization and validation as
separate downstream layers. Newer source contracts do not silently amend the
constitution; this RFC and its decision make the amendment explicit.

## 3. Model Admission and the Six Roles

Predictive identity, formed provenance, interaction distinction,
constitutive persistence, recoverable realization, and semantic identity
answer different questions. A proof of one does not establish the others.
Operational object certificates are sufficient in their declared classes,
not universal tests for all reality. A nontrivial object-discovery claim must
state what discrimination or explanatory gain is achieved against a baseline;
degenerate mathematical instances remain available for counterexample work.

If several roles are interpreted as aspects of one object, the application
must provide the relevant common source and interpretation or transport maps.
This is a positive matching obligation, not a demand for a new universal
identity relation. A body, predictive state, code, individual, and lineage
remain different mathematical types unless a particular bridge is provided.

In the regenerative population source, `actual` means produced by the specified
transition. Retained records and newborn parent records have that provenance.
Freshness relative to the old population retains `ValidIdentity` hypotheses;
temporal validity alone does not exclude duplicate initial IDs. No unrestricted
global uniqueness or real-world numerical identity follows from the record
construction. The reconciliation neither adds such a theorem nor weakens the
existing theorem's premises.

## 4. Same-Source and Normative Boundaries

Invoking a same-source result retains whichever transition, noise,
observation, history, action, memory, resource payment and arrival, clock,
generation record, and evaluation obligations its contract requires.
Pathwise, distributional, and observational equality have different strengths.
An explicit representation theorem can license transport; shared names,
matching marginals, or terminal outputs cannot substitute for it.

The current regenerative-population internal-execution result retains an
initial `ProgramPopulation θ` condition, preservation on reachable
populations, and its positive-branch hypothesis. An external `programPolicy θ`
on an arbitrary `State` is not by itself evidence of execution by the bodies'
own program. This qualification preserves the existing source theorem; it
introduces no new proof obligation for the already certified founder instance.

Paid repair, actual internal execution, and inherited programs can provide
nontrivial conditional explanations. They do not establish origin of all
supporting structures, unlimited maintenance without resources, universal
program discovery, or empirical identification. Expected population growth,
individual survival, viability, reflective purpose, and moral justification
must not be collapsed into one objective.

The philosophical and mathematical works therefore share a conditional
mechanism core and explicit interpretive connections. This is not an assertion
that every philosophical proposition is logically equivalent to a Lean type.
`Universal` does not assert that all reality has been explained or that UEOT is
the only possible foundational theory.

## 6. Cross-Domain Impact and Historical Inheritance

| Domain or consumer | Effect of adoption |
|---|---|
| Formal foundations | Admits the registered conditional classes without adding an unconditional feedback, optimization, or lifecycle axiom. Retains exact theorem hypotheses and countermodels. |
| Physics and cosmology | Preserves the existing Core/bridge/sector/physical-input/empirical distinctions. No mathematical isomorphism or named RG structure becomes physical equivalence by this decision. |
| Biology and evolution | May claim autopoiesis, regeneration, heredity, or selection only under its mechanism and empirical conditions. Growth is not automatically individual survival or adaptation. |
| Mind and consciousness | May use memory, control, or self-maintenance models without claiming they alone prove consciousness, reflective intent, or semantic meaning. |
| Meaning and philosophy | Preserves independent philosophical arguments and declares additional normative commitments. Mathematical optimization does not settle moral reasons. |
| Socioeconomics and civilization | States whose objective is used and how collective constraints enter. A market attractor or growth optimum is not automatically a social or moral optimum. |
| Legacy charters, STATE files, and memos | Preserve decisions and hypotheses at their historical dates. They cannot restore a superseded universal requirement for current Core admission. |

The companion resolution applies to the inherited strong readings in all six
`council/resolutions/20260311-*-charter.md` files and to their consumers in
`domains/`, including `STATE.md`, accepted claims, memos, and templates. It does
not retract every domain claim or erase its evidence. Only the unconditional
Core requirements identified here are superseded. A scoped domain hypothesis
can continue to be studied as such.

Current entry points should link the decision. Historical charters and memos
need not be rewritten. An `Active`, `Accepted`, or `OPEN` label in a preserved
record is not, by itself, evidence of current canonical status or an instruction
to reopen completed work.

## 7. Expected Monograph and Formalization Impact

The current philosophy and mathematics books already state the relevant
limitations. This change reconciles their use with governance; it does not
rewrite their main texts or generate new editions. It does not modify:

- the frozen Core v3.0 source or its 106 P-ID registry;
- the separate successor specification and its conditional contracts;
- production Lean definitions, theorem statements, imports, or proofs;
- Lean or Mathlib pins;
- accepted proof, execution, or publication receipts;
- physics working theories or GI material;
- empirical status or claims of theoretical priority.

No new Core theorem is required to implement this correction. Additional
application work or a stronger philosophical project must be separately
scoped and justified. Historical documents remain citable at their original
revision.

## 8. Implementation and Acceptance

The semantic package consists of four coordinated governance files plus this
RFC and its architect resolution. Current navigation and the audit closure
record should identify the adopted decision and unchanged mathematical scope.
`UEOT_CHANGE_CONTROL.md` retains its existing Class A requirements.

Before adoption, the lead integration should confirm:

1. all 12 canonical claim IDs, their order, and the four-field schema remain
   compatible; only allowed maturity labels are used;
2. Constitution, Glossary, Method, and registry agree on the old/new scope;
3. Type I/II and RG language do not restore a superseded universal rule;
4. the resolution's historical-inheritance clause reaches old charters and
   domain consumers, while preserving their provenance;
5. references and current entry-point links resolve;
6. the diff leaves frozen sources, production Lean, dependencies, and accepted
   evidence unchanged;
7. review findings and the actual adoption decision are recorded without
   claiming a fresh Lean execution or external human peer review.

These are targeted checks of this change, not a new proof campaign. Once the
lead's decision is recorded under the existing process, the governance gap is
closed. Independent replication, empirical discrimination, and new domain
questions remain separate work rather than automatic Core expansion.
