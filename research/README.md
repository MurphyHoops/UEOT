# UEOT Research

This directory holds exploratory work and auditable research records. A proposal
does not become canonical merely by appearing here; accepted changes are adopted
through the applicable source, proof and council-resolution process.

Target categories:

- `rfcs/` — proposed changes to Core, interfaces or governance
- `audits/` — independent consistency and assumption audits
- `prior-art/` — comparison with existing mathematics and physics
- `open-problems/` — explicit unresolved questions
- `derivations/` — exploratory derivations before promotion
- `migration/` — repository and specification migration records

Exploratory work may be ambitious. Its maturity label must remain explicit until it passes the appropriate promotion gate.

The [2026-11-13 Core audit closure](audits/core-closure-2026-11-13/README.md)
records a finite correction of claim scope, a source-matrix premise and evidence
presentation. Its [RFC](rfcs/20261113-core-claim-scope-reconciliation.md) is adopted
through the [corresponding resolution](../council/resolutions/20261113-core-claim-scope-reconciliation.md),
not by the audit's presence in this directory. Completed contracts and optional
or application work are explicitly distinguished.
