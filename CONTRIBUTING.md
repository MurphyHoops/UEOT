# Contributing to UEOT: copyright and licensing

UEOT welcomes scientific criticism, independent replication, research
contributions and reproducible negative results. The project has multiple
copyright/license categories; contributors must not assume all files are
licensed identically. See [LICENSE](LICENSE), [scope matrix](LICENSE_SCOPE.md)
and [copyright policy](COPYRIGHT.md).

Before submitting a pull request:

1. Identify the target file's license and whether it is historical CC BY
   4.0 research, future CC BY research, an edition-specific book,
   executable code, data, or third-party content.
2. Contribute only material you wrote or are authorized to submit under
   the target terms. Cite sources and disclose reused code, images,
   datasets and publisher materials with their actual licenses.
3. New original research prose and owned diagrams in the designated
   continuing-open areas are submitted under CC BY 4.0 unless a permitted
   exception is agreed and clearly recorded before release.
4. Do not assume a software license. Before new Lean/Python code is
   released for general reuse, verify its package license, contributor
   rights, third-party obligations and patent implications. Do not add
   Apache-2.0 headers to historical code without documented authority.
5. For new chapters or editions of the three books, identify previously
   CC BY passages, genuinely new content and third-party rights and
   consistently mark Markdown, LaTeX and PDF before publication.
6. Do not commit confidential UEOT-GI implementation, credentials,
   personal data or unlicensed experimental datasets and papers.
7. Preserve historical copyright and license notices.

Submitting a pull request does NOT automatically transfer ownership of
copyright to the project. This policy does not create a copyright
assignment or contributor license agreement.

For ambiguous content, clarify rights with a maintainer before release.
