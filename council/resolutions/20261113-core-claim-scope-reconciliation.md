## Decision

UEOT reconciles its constitutional vocabulary and model-admission rules with
the current conditional Core contracts and the public narrowing in the
philosophical and mathematical books. The four coordinated constitution files
and this resolution define the current scoped meanings. The earlier stronger
wording remains historically citable; it is not a current unconditional
mathematical requirement.

This decision addresses authority and semantic alignment. It does not find
that the existing conditional Lean theory is inconsistent or that different
meanings of Omega or Pi/Phi are the same predicate. It does not treat the
current books as having silently amended governance before this decision.

### 1. Objecthood and Identity — core-001 / core-002

The processual and relational interpretation of objects remains a philosophical
commitment. Mathematical candidates are admitted under declared source, task,
and certificate conditions. Predictive identity, formed provenance,
interaction distinction, constitutive persistence, recoverable
realization, and semantic identity retain separate meanings and hypotheses;
they do not universally imply one another. Applications that interpret them
as aspects of one object must supply the relevant common-source and
interpretation or transport evidence.

No operational certificate is adopted as a universal necessary-and-sufficient
test of real existence. Self-maintenance, recovery, heredity, and adaptation
remain additional claims where invoked. Degenerate models can remain valid
mathematical instances or counterexamples without proving a nontrivial
object-discovery claim.

### 2. Two Omega Uses — core-001

Organizational Omega is causal self-maintaining or self-regenerating feedback.
Operational Omega is the minimal-failure structure for a specified task,
horizon, intervention family, and execution semantics. Operational families
can be empty. Finite monotone reconstruction does not apply without its
hypotheses. The empty-family result does not, without an additional bridge,
refute every organizational use.

The old claim that every persistently existing object necessarily has an
Omega-loop no longer governs admission of every Core instance or every real
existent. A domain may defend a stronger, precisely stated necessity claim,
but it must provide its own premises, argument, and evidence.

### 3. Pi/Phi — core-003

Resource and informational organization remain legitimate qualitative
questions. A numerical Pi/Phi representation requires its declared response
family, anchors, units, factorization, and applicable assumptions. The old
universal coupled-optimization wording does not license an unconditional
common two-dimensional representation. High-rank and non-identifiability
results remain boundaries of the precise models they address, not a blanket
rejection of qualitative resource/information analysis.

### 4. Purpose, GOD, and GOA — core-004 / core-005

Conditional mechanisms can explain functional goal-directedness and its
consequences. The researcher's evaluation, an internally realized function,
reflective intent, and normative reason remain distinct. Growth or survival
does not establish moral value; normative conclusions require additional
premises and argument.

GOD claims retain their dynamics, admissible information and actions,
evaluation, horizon, and comparison class. GOA claims identify their long-run
type and the conditions establishing it. Optima can be non-unique or
unattained; invariant laws, attractors, periodic regimes, and conditioned
survivor laws need not coincide. No analysis must invent a unique GOD or GOA
when inapplicable or unestablished. Existence, globality, optimality, and
convergence must be proved or otherwise supported when claimed.

### 5. Lifecycle, Type Profiles, and Scale — core-006 / core-007

Emergence, maturation or optimization, and decay or dissolution are examined
where supported. The three phases are not a universal admissibility axiom.
Type I and Type II are qualified profiles, not an exhaustive binary taxonomy,
an inevitable developmental sequence, or another universal feedback rule.

Relevant multiscale relations remain mandatory to address. Object-scale
transport, process coarse-graining, and Wilsonian RG remain distinct unless an
explicit bridge connects them. Lifecycle change alone does not establish RG.

### 6. Same-Source, Actuality, and ValidIdentity

Generic compatibility does not prove one common realized body. Same-source
claims retain the transition, noise, observation, history, action, memory,
resource payment and arrival, clock, generation-record, and evaluation
obligations of their exact contracts. Required pathwise, distributional, and
observational relations may not be silently exchanged.

`Actual` in a formal model means generated by its declared mechanism; it does
not establish empirical validity. In the regenerative population source,
freshness relative to the old population retains the `ValidIdentity` inputs.
Temporal validity alone does not exclude duplicate initial IDs. The record
construction does not prove unrestricted global uniqueness or a universal
identity relation for real individuals.

Invoking the current regenerative-population internal-execution result
retains the initial `ProgramPopulation θ` condition, its preservation on
reachable populations, and the theorem's positive-branch hypothesis. An
external `programPolicy θ` on an arbitrary `State` does not by itself establish
execution of the bodies' own program.

### 7. Universal Scope and Frozen Mathematics

UEOT's supported scope includes a broad modeling language, lawful classes of
instances, and common conditional results. `Universal` does not certify an
explanation of all reality or a uniquely possible foundational theory.
Philosophy and mathematics share a conditional mechanism core and explicit
interpretive connections; they are not identical sets of proved propositions.

The frozen Core v3.0 source, 106 P-IDs, production Lean, toolchain and Mathlib
pins, and accepted evidence receipts remain unchanged. Successor contracts
remain separately identified. This decision neither supplies new formal
proofs nor promotes empirical support. No new Core research package is
activated by this reconciliation.

## Exact Supersession and Historical Inheritance

This decision supersedes the unconditional scope identified above in:

- the previous foundational commitments and invariants in
  `constitution/UEOT_CONSTITUTION.md`;
- the previous statements at `core-001` through `core-006`, with the explicit
  scope clarification at `core-007`, in the canonical claims registry;
- inherited meanings of the affected Glossary terms, including Type I/II and
  RG, and the previous universal reading of Method's required memo fields;
- uses of those requirements inherited through the six charter resolutions
  `council/resolutions/20260311-*-charter.md` and their consumers throughout
  `domains/`, including `STATE.md`, accepted claims, memos, and templates.

The six charters concern biology/evolution, formal foundations,
meaning/philosophy, mind/consciousness, physics/cosmos, and
socioeconomics/civilization. Their historical decisions and their scoped
domain hypotheses are preserved. This decision does not retract all of their
claims or erase objections; it governs which inherited requirements may be
used as current Core admission rules. Historical `Active`, `Accepted`, and
`OPEN` labels do not override it or automatically reopen later-completed work.

All 12 registry IDs, their order, and the four-field schema remain compatible.
Historical citations keep the meaning of the cited revision. A current
`canonical` entry records the adopted scoped commitment, not a proof of its
older stronger wording. Existing prohibitions on assumption import, status
inheritance, and silent term replacement remain in force.

## Cross-Domain Effect

Physics retains explicit bridge and sector-input obligations and its distinct
empirical validation requirements. Biological self-maintenance, consciousness,
semantic meaning, and social or moral value are not proved by this resolution.
Each can be studied through a declared domain model and additional argument or
evidence. Qualified positive maintenance and functional-purpose results remain
usable; this narrowing does not cancel them.

The Method template now requires applicability and boundary declarations.
It no longer forces missing structures into each memo. Current navigation
must identify this decision; the old charters, state files, and memos may
remain unchanged as historical provenance.

The fixed two-link quota is replaced by relevant cross-domain links with their
relation and evidence, or an explanation when none is relevant. This removes
an arbitrary output count; it does not waive any actual bridge required for a
claim or license an unsupported cross-domain inference.
