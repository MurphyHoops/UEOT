# UEOT Glossary

## Scope

Protected vocabulary is used with the scope adopted in the
[Core claim reconciliation](../council/resolutions/20261113-core-claim-scope-reconciliation.md).
The active Core contract determines the exact mathematical type and hypotheses.
These entries neither replace those contracts nor turn philosophical
interpretations into theorems.

## Core Terms

### Omega-loop

In an organizational explanation, a causal feedback structure whose operation
maintains or regenerates a specified pattern. The mechanism, boundary,
disturbances, resources, and persistence criterion must be identified. Calling
something persistent does not establish such a loop. This organizational use
is distinct from operational `Omega` below and is not a universal admission
requirement for every Core model or every real existent.

### Operational Omega

The minimal failing deletions or interventions for a specified certificate,
horizon, mechanism family, and execution semantics. The family may be empty.
Finite monotone failure permits the corresponding minimal-cut reconstruction;
non-monotone interventions require their full response or an appropriate
alternative contract. An empty operational family alone neither establishes
nor refutes organizational self-regeneration. A bridge is needed to identify
the two uses.

### Object

Philosophically, a distinguishable concrete organization considered through
its formation, relations, persistence, and change. Mathematically, a candidate
admitted under a stated task, source, and certificate. Neither description is
an unqualified necessary-and-sufficient criterion for every real existent.
Events and other entities are not declared unreal because they lack a
persistence certificate. Self-maintenance, recovery, heredity, and adaptation
require additional applicable claims.

### Object Roles

The current Core distinguishes the following roles:

| Role | Question and required boundary |
|---|---|
| Predictive identity | Which histories have the same future response law under the declared protocols, observations, and permissions? |
| Formed provenance | What actual generation image, relation, seed, or cover produced the candidate? |
| Interaction distinction | Which permitted probes distinguish candidates, under the declared response and error criterion? |
| Constitutive persistence | Which actual source, initial conditions, constitutive conditions, and persistence or safety certificate sustain the candidate? |
| Recoverable realization | Which remaining information, interpreter, physical intervention, and cost permit recovery? |
| Semantic identity | Which explicitly declared identity relation is preserved by the specified interpretation or transport? |

No row universally entails the other rows. Treating several as properties of
one object requires the relevant common-source and interpretation evidence.
Historical continuation, similarity, predictive equivalence, and strict
numerical identity must not be used interchangeably.

### Actual / Realized

Within a formal model, generated by its declared transition or mechanism, as
opposed to supplied only as an independent desired output. This can establish
model-internal provenance and causal consequences. It does not mean that the
model has been empirically identified or that its interpretation is the only
possible account of a real system.

### Identity Records and ValidIdentity

The regenerative population construction records founder and birth history,
retains declared identities for retained members, and records actual parentage
for generated newborns. Its freshness theorem requires the stated
`ValidIdentity` inputs for the old population. Such temporal validity does not
by itself exclude duplicate initial identities. A claim of global uniqueness
must provide the corresponding initial and transition conditions. These
records do not constitute a universal theory of real-world numerical identity.

### Internal Program Execution

In the regenerative population model, the current internal-execution result
uses the initial `ProgramPopulation θ` condition, its preservation on
reachable populations, and the theorem's positive-branch hypothesis.
Applying an external `programPolicy θ` to an arbitrary `State` does not by
itself establish execution of the bodies' own program. The declared founder
construction provides the relevant initial program condition for its positive
instance; it is not a property of all arbitrary population states.

### Same Source

A common generative basis, or the explicitly licensed transport from it,
sufficient for the contracts being combined. Relevant obligations may include
transition and noise laws, observations, history updates, available actions
and memory, cost and resource-arrival timing, clock, generation records, and
evaluation. Pathwise, distributional, and observational agreement have
different strengths. Same terminology, equal marginals, or matching terminal
outputs do not establish whichever stronger relation a theorem requires.

### Pi

A qualitative concern with resources, physical efficiency, economy, payoff,
or viability. A numerical `Pi` is a model-specific quantity whose operational
meaning, units, feasible class, and evaluation must be declared. These choices
are not automatically interchangeable or universally optimized.

### Phi

A qualitative concern with information, observation, uncertainty, learning, or
organization. A numerical `Phi` requires a declared information structure,
task, and operational interpretation. Information, memory, prediction,
control, and meaning are not identical merely because they are discussed
under this heading.

### Pi/Phi Representation

A particular response-factorization or coordinate model, not a consequence of
the qualitative resource/information distinction. It must identify the
response family, anchors, domain, and applicable linear, smooth, or other
conditions. There is no unconditional common two-dimensional representation
of all Core systems or all real phenomena.

### GOD

Global Optimal Direction, a historical name retained for a precisely stated
direction or optimal-action claim relative to dynamics, allowable actions and
information, evaluation, horizon, and comparison class. A set of optima may
contain ties or be empty when an optimum is not attained. The name does not
prove global optimality, uniqueness, internal computability, or convergence.
A local result must remain local; some analyses have no applicable `GOD`.

### GOA

Global Optimal Attractor, a historical name requiring an explicit long-run
type and contract. An invariant law, attractor, periodic regime, growth
criterion, or conditioned survivor law are different mathematical objects and
need not be unique, optimal, or mutually equivalent. Any optimality or
convergence claim must be separately established under its stated evaluation
and hypotheses. Some analyses have no applicable `GOA`.

### Purpose and Normativity

A function may be explained through a mechanism's contribution to declared
maintenance, regeneration, or other consequences. This explanation is distinct
from a researcher's chosen objective, a subject's reflective intention, and a
reason for what ought to be valued. Expected population growth, individual
survival, physical viability, and moral good are not interchangeable criteria.
Normative conclusions require explicit additional premises and argument.

### Loop Complexity (`L_c`)

A declared diagnostic or profile of organizational structure, nesting,
realization, or failure dependence. The model must state which diagnostic is
used and its units, baseline, and domain. No universal scalar of organization,
adaptation, consciousness, or value is assumed.

### Loop Openness (`L_o`)

A declared diagnostic or profile of coupling to external matter, energy,
information, or supporting mechanisms. Different flows and interfaces need
not share a common scalar. The boundary, units, timing, and any normalization
conditions must be given; externally supported maintenance is not thereby
resource-independent self-maintenance.

### Type I Object

A historical qualitative profile emphasizing relatively stable or passive
persistence under declared boundaries and disturbances. Internal regulatory
closure is an additional mechanism claim where supported, not a condition
imposed on every persistent instance. The relevant persistence and maintenance
certificates must be named.

### Type II Object

A historical qualitative profile emphasizing environmental coupling,
endogenous maintenance, or adaptive capacity when established. Resource
exchange does not alone prove autonomy or adaptation. Type I and Type II are
not an exhaustive, disjoint binary classification, an inevitable lifecycle,
or a normative hierarchy; a model may show several profiles or neither.

### Dynamical Mode

Temporal behavior under specified dynamics and observation: for example
stationary, periodic, transient, or chaotic. These categories are not asserted
to be exhaustive or disjoint in every description. No dynamical mode alone
proves an optimization phase, adaptation, or a unique attractor.

### RG Flow

A qualified scale-dependent construction. Object-scale representation or
organization transport, process coarse-graining, and Wilsonian
renormalization-group flow must be distinguished. Each requires its own maps,
variables, and preservation or approximation conditions. Lifecycle change is
not automatically an RG flow, and a shared name does not establish a physical
bridge between these constructions.

### Research Memo

The primary unit of domain research output. A memo states its question, source,
task, applicable constitutional terms, cross-domain links, and testable or
actionable consequences. Inapplicable or unestablished structures are recorded
as such, with reasons, rather than supplied merely to fill a template.

### Cross-Review

A structured critique intended to expose tension, missing bridges, or
integration opportunities. It identifies the exact claim, evidence, scope,
and response required. Agreement between agents does not substitute for proof
or independent empirical validation.

### Architect Resolution

A binding written ruling from the chief architect that accepts, rejects,
splits, or defers claims and sets constraints under the change-control
process. A proposed resolution is a candidate for that ruling, not evidence
that adoption, publication, or a merge has occurred.
