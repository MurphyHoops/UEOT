# UEOT Constitution

## Purpose

This document defines the protected constitutional commitments, scope rules,
and change control of UEOT. Memos, reviews, resolutions, and book chapters must
remain consistent with this layer unless a formal RFC and architect resolution
explicitly changes it.

The scope reconciliation is specified in
[the 2026-11-13 RFC](../research/rfcs/20261113-core-claim-scope-reconciliation.md)
and its [architect resolution](../council/resolutions/20261113-core-claim-scope-reconciliation.md).
These documents identify the previous and revised meanings; retaining a claim
ID does not make its historical, stronger wording a current theorem.

## Canonical Routing and Epistemic Layers

UEOT is maintained as a derivational research program with distinct authority
layers:

1. `constitution/` governs protected concepts and change control.
2. `core/` contains the active domain-neutral theory and mathematical source
   specifications.
3. `formalization/` contains machine proofs of identified Core statements.
4. `physics/` and other realization layers may add explicitly declared sector
   assumptions and bridge theorems.
5. `validation/` records benchmark, experimental, and falsification evidence.
6. `publications/` are snapshots of a theory state and do not override a newer
   approved Core specification or proof ledger.

No layer may silently inherit the epistemic status of another. A constitutional
commitment is not automatically a theorem; a Lean theorem is not automatically
a physical law; an empirical fit is not automatically a proof of the generic
Core. A mathematical existence proof does not by itself establish real-world
existence or practical realizability.

Repository-wide maturity labels are: `canonical`, `proved`, `partial`,
`pending`, `conjecture`, `empirical`, and `deprecated`. In the canonical claims
registry, `canonical` records an adopted commitment or rule with its stated
scope; it does not confer `proved` or `empirical` status.

## Foundational Commitments

1. **Objecthood is interpreted processually and relationally.**
   UEOT investigates how distinguishable organizations form, persist, change,
   and interact. This is a philosophical orientation, not a proof that every
   real entity satisfies one operational object certificate. In mathematics,
   object admission follows the declared source, task, and contract. Persistent
   self-maintenance is an additional claim to establish when it is used.

2. **Persistence and Omega must be typed.**
   An organizational `Omega`-loop denotes causal feedback that maintains or
   regenerates a pattern. An operational `Omega` denotes minimal failures of
   a specified certificate under specified deletions or interventions. Neither
   meaning is silently substituted for the other. Operational families may be
   empty; minimal-cut reconstruction retains its finite and monotone-failure
   hypotheses. Self-regenerating feedback is not an unconditional admission
   requirement for every Core model or every real existent. A stronger
   domain-specific necessity claim requires its own argument and evidence.

3. **Pi and Phi organize questions; numerical reductions require hypotheses.**
   Resource, viability, efficiency, and informational organization are useful
   qualitative concerns. Their names do not imply that all systems optimize
   two quantities or that all responses factor through a common pair of
   coordinates. A numerical `Pi/Phi` model must state its response family,
   operational anchors, units, factorization, and applicable regularity and
   parameter assumptions. Linear and smooth representations have their own
   conditions and counterexamples. High-rank and non-identifiability results
   are boundaries of the corresponding precise claims, not contradictions of
   every possible qualitative use of these terms.

4. **Functional purpose is a conditional naturalistic explanation.**
   UEOT seeks explanations of goal-directed functioning in mechanisms,
   maintenance, inheritance, and their consequences, without introducing an
   external teleological force. A supplied performance criterion, an internally
   realized function, a reflective intention, and a normative reason are
   different claims. Proving optimization of an assigned criterion does not
   prove that it is the object's unique purpose or that it is morally good.
   Normative conclusions must declare and defend their additional premises.

5. **GOD and GOA are evaluation- and model-relative when applicable.**
   A `GOD` claim identifies directions or actions relative to stated dynamics,
   feasible actions, information, evaluation, and comparison class. It may be
   set-valued, unattained, or inapplicable. A `GOA` claim states the type of
   long-run object being studied and the hypotheses that establish it. An
   invariant law, an attractor, a periodic regime, and a conditioned survivor
   law are not interchangeable. Neither uniqueness, convergence, nor optimality
   follows from the name. Domain work must assess applicability; it need not
   manufacture a unique `GOD` or `GOA` for every object.

6. **Lifecycle analysis is conditional on the phenomenon.**
   Emergence, maturation or optimization, and decay or dissolution are useful
   questions when supported by the model or observations. They are not three
   mandatory phases through which every admissible object must pass. Static,
   cyclic, persistent, and transient models are evaluated under their actual
   contracts. An optimization phase must not be inferred merely from survival
   or elapsed time.

7. **Multiscale reasoning is required where applicable.**
   Analyses must address relevant sub-object relations, super-object boundary
   conditions, and changes of description. Object-scale transport, process
   coarse-graining, and Wilsonian RG are distinct unless connected by an
   explicit bridge. Type I and Type II are qualified organizational profiles,
   not an exhaustive binary classification or an alternative universal
   feedback requirement.

8. **UEOT remains criticizable without requiring endless Core expansion.**
   Missing equations, unresolved conjectures, counterexamples, and incomplete
   evidence must be stated. Conditional Core closure concerns the registered
   claims and their interfaces, not completeness about all reality. `Universal`
   names an ambition for broad language, lawful instantiation, and common
   conditional results; it does not certify explanation of every existent or
   uniqueness among possible foundations.

## Model Admission, Object Roles, and Shared Sources

The current Core distinguishes predictive identity, formed provenance,
interaction distinction, constitutive persistence, recoverable
realization, and semantic identity. Each answers a different question and has
its own hypotheses. None universally implies the other five. A body, a
predictive state, a program, an individual, and a lineage must not be equated
merely because their records use the same name.

An application interpreting several roles as aspects of one object must give
the common source and interpretation or transport maps needed by the claims it
uses. A generic assembly of compatible statements is not by itself proof that
they describe one realized body. Invoking a same-source theorem requires its
actual source, transition or noise law, observations and histories, action and
memory permissions, resource costs and arrival times, clock, generation
records, and evaluation conditions, to the extent specified by that theorem.
The required equality or preservation may be pathwise, distributional, or
observational; a weaker one must not replace a stronger one without a bridge.

`Actual` or `realized` in a formal model means generated by the specified
transition or mechanism. It does not assert empirical verification of a real
system. In the regenerative population model, retained identity and actual
parent records follow the declared record construction; freshness relative to
the old population requires the theorem's `ValidIdentity` inputs. Those
inputs alone do not establish uniqueness of every initial population. No
general theorem of real-world numerical identity follows from these records.

To invoke the current internal-execution result for the regenerative
population model, retain the initial `ProgramPopulation θ` condition, its
preservation on reachable populations, and the theorem's positive-branch
hypothesis. Applying an external `programPolicy θ` to an arbitrary `State`
does not by itself establish execution of the bodies' own program.

Operational object certificates are sufficient within their registered scope,
not an unqualified necessary-and-sufficient test for all reality. Nontrivial
object-discovery claims must state their discriminating task and baseline.
Degenerate models may still be legitimate mathematical instances or
countermodels. No model acquires self-maintenance, recovery, heredity,
adaptation, or normative value merely by being called an object.

## Constitutional Invariants

The following may not be overwritten by domain agents:

- protected terms retain their declared meaning, type, and applicability;
- philosophical commitments, mathematical assumptions and conclusions,
  empirical claims, and normative premises remain distinguishable;
- theorem hypotheses, quantifiers, and counterexample boundaries are retained;
- shared-source and representation claims require the stated bridge evidence;
- relevant lifecycle and multiscale questions are assessed without forcing
  inapplicable structures into a model;
- objections and historical provenance remain accessible;
- canonical changes follow the written change-control process.

## Permitted Extensions

Domain agents may:

- restate the Core in domain-specific language with an explicit interpretation;
- propose domain-specific objects, loops, and `Pi/Phi` quantities;
- establish applicable `GOD/GOA`, lifecycle, and multiscale claims;
- propose mathematical formalizations;
- define diagnostics, experiments, and falsification paths;
- identify tensions with external theories or with a proposed UEOT claim.

Domain agents may not:

- rewrite protected meanings or strengthen their quantifiers unilaterally;
- declare conjectural claims canonical without resolution;
- import domain- or sector-specific assumptions into the generic Core without
  explicit reclassification;
- claim a physical result follows from generic UEOT when an unlisted bridge or
  sector-selection assumption is required;
- infer normativity or empirical identity from formal optimization or labels;
- erase unresolved objections;
- silently replace UEOT language with another framework.

## Frozen Sources and Further Research

This reconciliation does not rewrite the frozen Core v3.0 source, its 106 P-IDs,
accepted production theorems, or proof receipts. Successor contracts remain
separately identified; publication edition numbers do not create a new Core
version. An application-specific limitation, an old `OPEN` record superseded
by later evidence, or a stronger research ambition does not automatically
reopen a completed Core task. A new task must identify its new counterevidence,
unmet consumer obligation, or separately stated research goal.

## Constitutional Change Rule

Any modification to this file requires:

1. a written RFC;
2. an explicit statement of the old and proposed rule;
3. rationale and evidence basis;
4. cross-domain impact analysis;
5. an architect resolution stored in `council/resolutions/`.

Without that flow, constitutional edits are invalid.
