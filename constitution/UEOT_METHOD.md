# UEOT Method

## Standard Analytical Frame

Every domain research pass should answer the following in order:

1. What question, source, environment, observation, time scale, and comparison
   task are being studied? Which statements are philosophical, mathematical,
   empirical, or normative?
2. What candidate object and object roles are used? What admits the candidate
   under the stated contract, and what evidence connects its different roles?
3. Is maintenance or regeneration claimed? If so, which organizational
   `Omega` mechanisms or operational failure structures are used, with what
   interventions, horizon, resource conditions, and assumptions?
4. Are `Pi` and `Phi` qualitative concerns or numerical quantities? For a
   numerical representation, what responses, anchors, factorization, and
   regularity conditions make it applicable?
5. Are `GOD` or `GOA` claims applicable? If so, what are the evaluation,
   admissible information and actions, comparison class, long-run type, and
   existence, attainment, uniqueness, or convergence conditions?
6. What lifecycle or multiscale questions are supported by this phenomenon?
   Which scale maps or RG construction are actually used?
7. What external theories support, challenge, or compete with the framing?
   What is standard, newly defined, newly proved, or still only conjectured?
8. What counterexamples, testable claims, or concrete research actions follow?

Applicability is part of the answer. `Not applicable`, `not established`, an
empty failure family, several optima, or absence of a proved limit may be the
correct result. Such answers require a reason and an exact boundary; they are
not defects to be hidden by adding an assumed loop, objective, or lifecycle.
These descriptive answers do not create new registry maturity labels.

## Required Output Shapes

### Research Memo

Each memo must contain:

- question, source, scope, and epistemic role of the proposed claims;
- constitutional primitives used, with meanings and applicability;
- domain object framing and the relevant object-role contracts;
- `Omega` analysis or a reasoned statement of inapplicability or open evidence;
- `Pi/Phi` analysis with qualitative and numerical uses distinguished;
- applicable `GOD/GOA` derivation or the precise reason it is unavailable;
- lifecycle and multiscale interpretation where supported, with scope stated;
- external literature, prior-art distinctions, objections, and countermodels;
- relevant cross-domain links with their relation and evidential status, or
  an explanation when none is relevant;
- research consequences and explicit failure or revision conditions.

A cross-domain link may identify a limitation or a non-transfer result; it need
not assert a successful unification. There is no numerical link quota, and
relevant bridge obligations are not waived when no additional link is useful.
The required headings do not make every
named structure a necessary property of every model. Type I/II terminology or
an inherited domain template may not reinstate a universal feedback, binary
classification, optimization, or lifecycle requirement.

### Cross-Review

Each review must identify:

- the exact claim, revision, and applicable contract under review;
- the relation type: support, tension, bridge, critique, or dependency;
- the evidence and any mismatch of meanings, hypotheses, or quantifiers;
- what the target domain must respond to.

### Architect Resolution

Each resolution must identify:

- what was decided and the applicable change class;
- the old and new rule for a constitutional change;
- the evidence, affected claims, and cross-domain impact;
- what remains unresolved;
- follow-up work by domain, including when no new mathematical work is needed;
- what becomes canonical versus provisional;
- the scope of supersession and the historical records that remain provenance.

A proposed resolution must not describe itself as adopted or merged. Adoption
follows the existing constitutional change process; semantic correction does
not require an additional approval procedure beyond that process.

## Evidence Discipline

- The constitution governs protected concepts and change control.
- The active `core/` specification is the source layer for domain-neutral
  mathematical claims. Frozen sources and successor contracts retain their
  own versions and identifiers.
- Historical papers, charters, accepted memos, and state records preserve
  their contemporary decisions; they do not override a later explicit
  reconciliation or a newer approved mathematical contract.
- Lean formalization certifies the formal statement actually imported and
  built. Semantic matching to the source claim is a separate obligation.
- A proof's type, hypotheses, quantifiers, dependencies, and applicability
  matter more than theorem counts or successful compilation alone.
- Source-bound existing proof receipts may be cited with their actual status;
  a prose reconciliation is not a new Lean build or proof result.
- External literature is used for prior-art control, validation, stress
  testing, translation into domain language, and empirical benchmarking.
- Standard results and borrowed methods must not be presented as original
  merely because they are renamed or imported into a new formalization.
- Unsupported grand claims remain provisional. Analogies do not count as
  validation, and a permissive mathematical language is not by itself a
  universal explanation of reality.
- Physical constants, symmetry assumptions, quantum-sector selection
  principles, effective-theory assumptions, and empirical calibration inputs
  must be declared rather than attributed to generic UEOT Core.
- Normative premises and evaluative choices must be stated. Formal growth,
  persistence, or optimization does not establish moral justification.

## Shared-Source and Identity Discipline

When combining claims about one object, identify the common source or licensed
transport and check the consumer theorem's obligations. These can include
transition and noise laws, observations, history updates, allowed actions and
memory, payments and resource arrival, time, identity and generation records,
and evaluation. State whether the relation is pathwise, distributional, or
observational. Agreement of marginals or terminal outputs does not establish
stronger action-history or whole-path agreement.

Distinguish a generic conjunction of interfaces from an actual common-source
instance. Do not silently grant a controller the analyst's information, change
resource timing, replace an action class, or exchange finite-horizon and
all-time strategy quantifiers. A proved transport may license a change; the
transport and its hypotheses must be named.

In the regenerative population construction, actual output and parent records
come from its declared transition. Freshness relative to the old population
uses the stated `ValidIdentity` assumptions; temporal validity does not by
itself prove duplicate-free initial identities or global uniqueness. These
are model-internal statements, not an empirical discovery of real numerical
identity. The relevant interpretations must preserve this distinction.

To invoke the current internal-execution result for the regenerative
population model, retain the initial `ProgramPopulation θ` condition, its
preservation on reachable populations, and the theorem's positive-branch
hypothesis. Applying an external `programPolicy θ` to an arbitrary `State`
does not by itself establish execution of the bodies' own program.

## Monograph Discipline

Book chapter prose may only be promoted to canonical draft status when:

- supporting memos exist;
- counterarguments have been recorded;
- architect review has occurred;
- unresolved gaps are named explicitly.

Philosophical argument and mathematical theorem may support one another through
an explicit interpretation without becoming logically equivalent. A book must
not turn that relationship into a claim that all of its philosophy has been
proved in Lean.

## Promotion Gates

### Core theorem to proved

A Core claim may be marked `proved` only when its source statement is
identified, the formal statement is semantically matched, the theorem is
included in an official build target, the pinned build passes, prohibited proof
holes/axioms are absent, and the coverage ledger is updated.

### Core to physical realization

A physical derivation must list the Core dependencies, bridge theorem or
conjecture, sector-specific assumptions, and external physical inputs. Same-name
structures at different scales are not identified without a bridge theorem.
Mathematical isomorphism alone does not establish empirical equivalence.

### Physical claim to empirical status

A claim requires a defined observable, comparison/null model, data or hardware
provenance, uncertainty/statistical procedure, and explicit failure criterion.

## Bounded Research and Closure

Conditional closure is assessed against the registered claims and interfaces.
Do not reopen a superseded historical task or create new Core proof work merely
to increase coverage counts. New work must identify a concrete counterexample,
unmet consumer obligation, or separately scoped research goal, and explain why
an existing theorem, an explicit assumption, or narrower wording is insufficient.
Application-specific hypotheses and philosophical disputes do not automatically
become missing Core mathematics.
