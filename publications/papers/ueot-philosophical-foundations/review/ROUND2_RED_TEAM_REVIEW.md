## 2. Resolution of the blocking issue

### B1 — resolved

The decisive changes are in §§5.1 and 5.4 and the first paragraph of the conclusion.

- The majority routine's causal ability to restore code is distinguished from the additional basis for calling restoration its function.
- That basis is identified as the model's specified design task and its relation to the pre-damage programme retained in copies. It is not inferred from the mere occurrence of an output.
- The manuscript explicitly separates descriptive causal contribution, grounded functional attribution, intrinsic biological normativity, and moral or practical reasons.
- It treats the model as demonstrating the first level and supporting a designed-function instance of the second. It expressly leaves intrinsic biological normativity unresolved.
- The population calculation is now called a demographic performance explanation unless further evidence establishes growth as a function or target of the bearer.
- Corti's objection is stated as an objection about biological functional correctness and malfunction, not merely moral goodness. The manuscript acknowledges rather than claims to solve it.

This meets the prior acceptance condition. There is now an identifiable extra ground for the positive purposive attribution; the demographic calculation is not silently included under that attribution. The conclusion retains this restriction instead of recovering the stronger claim after the qualifications.

No argument that designed function establishes intrinsic biological purpose is required for the revised thesis. Its refusal to make that inference is an appropriate limit, not an unacknowledged failure.
