## 1. Actual verdict

**MAJOR REVISION before presenting this version as ready for philosophical journal submission.** The manuscript contains a viable, carefully bounded research argument, and its substantive examples make it more than an inventory of qualifications. Nevertheless, its central discussion of purpose still crosses a distinction that the paper itself has correctly established. The closest contemporary competitors are acknowledged accurately but not engaged closely enough to settle the claimed additional contribution.

This verdict does **not** report an invented reference, a demonstrated failure of the growth theorem, an unsupported claim of a complete Lean proof, or a failure to meet the word requirement. Those would be different problems. The main defect is philosophical: causal contribution, independently grounded function, intrinsic biological normativity, and moral justification remain insufficiently separated at the point where the paper draws its positive purposive conclusion.

The problem is repairable without writing a different paper or solving all of biological normativity. A narrower positive claim, a direct comparison with the strongest recent alternatives, and a precise statement of the preservation argument's scope would support a defensible methodological and philosophical contribution. I do not recommend restoring a stronger universal ontology to make the result appear more ambitious.

## 2. Blocking issue

### B1. The positive purpose conclusion still conflates descriptive success with the ground of a purposive or normative attribution

**Location:** §5.1, especially the paragraph beginning “The finite source contains both kinds of relation”; §5.4, particularly the paragraphs beginning “The remaining question” and “A modest purposive explanation”; conclusion's first paragraph. In the frozen Markdown, the most acute transition is at line 229.

The paper correctly says that a growth criterion can be achieved without being the bearer's goal. It then places two explanatory achievements together: the repair mechanism explains restored execution, and the population model explains demographic consequences. The next sentence says that these explanations have content because they exclude alternatives and can fail under perturbations, followed by “Calling them purposive need not attribute consciousness or moral standing.”

Excluding causal alternatives and having counterfactual failure conditions establish explanatory content; they do not establish purposiveness. The demographic explanation can remain an entirely non-purposive account of consequences. Even the repair description requires an identified basis for treating the restored configuration as successful rather than merely as the state produced. The model offers such a basis through its design specification and historical copying relation, but it does not thereby establish intrinsic biological normativity.

The treatment of Corti does not yet resolve this. His target is the inference from causal conditions of existence to objective **biological functional norms**, including the move from internal dependence to intrinsic norms. It is not merely the stronger move to categorical goodness or moral obligation. Saying that an organization need not be categorically good leaves his central objection unanswered. See Corti 2023, §3, especially the simple-dependence and closed-dependence arguments: <https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-023-04255-7>.

**Required revision:** distinguish at least (a) a descriptive contribution to a specified capacity, (b) an attribution grounded by a design, selection, or regulatory account, (c) intrinsic biological normativity, and (d) moral reasons. The paper need not defend all four. State which it actually defends in the device. Restrict the immediate positive conclusion to implemented restoration under the model's identified standard. Call the growth result a demographic or performance explanation unless the additional ground for a purpose attribution is supplied. Acknowledge that Corti's objection remains open for an inference to intrinsic norms.

**Acceptance condition:** a reader must be able to identify a premise beyond causal effectiveness that licenses each occurrence of “for,” “purpose,” and “functional success” in the main positive argument. Alternatively, the paper must explicitly use a weaker descriptive sense and relinquish the stronger conclusion. Removing consciousness and moral standing alone does not satisfy this condition.

## 3. Important revisions

### I1. Engage Bich and Cuciniello as positions that issue verdicts on the case

**Location:** introduction's precedent paragraph; §5.4; §6.2, particularly the paragraph beginning “Organizational teleology presents the closest challenge.”

The references are real and the existing summaries are fair. But saying that these authors already connect organization and purpose does not yet show what the present account adds to their explanations.

Bich's issue is not simply that mechanisms matter. His regulatory account concerns state-sensitive control that coordinates which activities of constraints occur, in service of the organism's organization. The paper should ask what the modeled diagnosis, repair, and execution gate establish under that account. Its trusted bootstrap and externally supplied resources are not automatically disqualifying, but the device has not been shown to meet the whole organizational account. The preservation analysis can test the adequacy of a representation used in that account without being a rival account of intrinsic biological purpose. Relevant primary text: Bich 2024, §§4–5, <https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/rati.12405>.

Cuciniello's distinctive result is the difference between the naturalizability of a stable minimal organization and the further difficulties introduced by regulative organizational change. The concluding question concerns where regulatory goals come from, including historical and cross-generational organization. A fixed-code restoration model does not settle that problem. Its separate token and lineage records do, however, let the paper specify a distinction that a claimed transfer between such explanations must respect. Relevant primary text: Cuciniello 2026, §§4–6, <https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40656-026-00731-8>.

**Recommended revision:** replace part of the broad comparison in §6.2 with a direct comparison of (1) the attribution each account aims to establish, (2) what it would recognize in the device, (3) what remains unproved, and (4) the representational error the preservation analysis can diagnose. Do not suggest that either author overlooks mechanisms, time, or lineage. Approximately two concentrated paragraphs would do more work than several additional general acknowledgments.

### I2. State that the fibre condition addresses exact attribution from the stated representation, not every form of explanation

**Location:** §3.3 and §7.2; abstract and introduction's universal formulation of explanatory transfer.

The set-theoretic factorization statement is correct. The paper also commendably says it is elementary, necessary rather than sufficient, and vulnerable to artificially adding the desired conclusion as a feature. The remaining risk is its philosophical quantification. Explanations may be probabilistic, approximate, partial, or population-level without preserving the realized token-level value of every explanandum.

For example, let two source states have repair fees 0 and 3, and let a quotient merge them. The realized fee does not factor through that quotient. Nevertheless, if a justified distribution assigns probability one half to each state, the quotient plus that distribution can determine the expected fee 1.5 and the fee distribution. This does not recover the individual's repair history; it can still support an aggregate cost explanation. The paper's strongest example concerns the latter-to-former transfer, not the impossibility of all cost explanation from a coarse model.

**Recommended revision:** state explicitly that the elementary condition concerns exact, bearer-specific attributions made from the representation alone. If the relevant feature is a probability law, expected value, or approximate relation, define preservation for that feature. If auxiliary evidence is added, apply the condition to the combined representation. This would turn the already mentioned regularity qualification into a clear philosophical scope restriction.

It would also help to state the target before selecting the representation: bearer, explanatory contrast, allowed intervention class, time horizon, and basis for evaluation. These choices should be justified by the inquiry, not selected after seeing which partition makes the conclusion true. The draft already gestures toward this; the final statement should make it explicit.

### I3. Explain the extra content of joint realization over separate existence claims

**Location:** §5.2's attainment discussion and §6.1's “successful alternative.”

The positive construction is the manuscript's best response to the objection that preservation is a checklist. But its added logical content can be made sharper. Demonstrating one possible repairing system and one possible optimizing system does not establish a system that does both under the same restrictions. Here the damaged founder, programme, resource transactions, and stochastic source supply a shared realization for the two claims.

That distinction is already present in prose, but the reader is asked to infer exactly what is shared and what remains merely separately stipulated. A compact statement should name the common bearer, source, admissible actions, and evaluation. It should then state that the conjunction establishes compatibility, without establishing a new ground of intrinsic purpose or universal realization.

This would give the positive part more argumentative weight. The conjunction is meaningful when independently specified demands might conflict. If all relevant features are only chosen after designing an accommodating toy, non-vacuity alone is too cheap to bear the claimed philosophical significance. Explain which constraints were inherited from the declared problem and which were modeling choices.

### I4. Clarify the target of the allegedly tempting invalid inference

**Location:** §6.1, paragraph beginning “Consider the tempting inference.”

The reconstruction is useful, but no current external opponent is shown to infer individual identity and natural purpose solely from a birth quotient plus population optimality. Computational mechanics need not claim token identity; organizational theorists need not define intrinsic goals by maximal population growth. They would reject the inference for their own reasons.

**Recommended revision:** identify whether this is (a) an inference made or invited by a specific historical UEOT passage, (b) a conditional countermodel to a proposed unification, or (c) a demonstrable problem in another actual application. If it is (b), label it as such. Do not let a constructed error appear to be a demonstrated failing of the closest literature.

The revision need not enlarge the literature. It needs a clear statement of the explanatory task and the audience whose inference is being corrected. The strongest contribution is likely a source-based reconstruction of UEOT plus a general diagnostic that other accounts may accept, rather than a refutation of those accounts.

### I5. Keep supplied inputs distinct from established causal interventions

**Location:** §3.1, sentence citing Shalizi–Crutchfield and Barnett–Crutchfield.

The mathematical ancestry is correctly attributed. The expression “interventions or supplied inputs” should nevertheless avoid implying that conditioning on future inputs automatically establishes an intervention semantics. An input-output transducer represents conditional response structure; treating an input as a causal intervention depends on the source and the relation between input choice and noise.

**Recommended revision:** describe the cited construction as an input-output extension, then say that the present source's declared protocol semantics supply whatever causal interpretation is intended. The later exclusion of anticipatory access to current noise is a useful part of that explanation. This is a precision issue, not evidence that the cited transducer result is wrong.

## 4. Optional editorial improvements

1. **Reduce repetition of disclaimers.** The distinction between a finite mathematical construction, empirical realization, intrinsic purpose, and moral justification is repeated in §§4–7. Keep it explicit at the relevant inference, then use the saved space for the close comparisons above. Do not remove the transparent evidence disclosure.
2. **Trim peripheral comparisons if space is needed.** The FEP and causal-emergence paragraphs are correctly bounded. They contribute less to this paper than direct engagement with the organizational accounts. They can remain short or be moved to notes.
3. **Make the model's first introduction easier to enter.** The 2/3/4 classification is a strong organizing result, but its initial “original positive regime” refers forward to parameters not yet introduced. A one-sentence informal explanation of the birth advantage and repair resource constraint would reduce the load on readers without adding a second formal exposition.
4. **Choose a consistent level of realism.** The manuscript supports defeasible realism about particular causal organizations. It does not need to imply that all genuine explanation requires one general relational ontology. The current explicit qualifications mostly achieve this; preserve them in the abstract and conclusion.
5. **Keep historical identity modest.** The marked-token convention makes the source distinctions exact; it does not resolve natural fission, fusion, or organismal individuation. The draft presently handles this well. Do not strengthen the conclusion during revision.

## 7. Revision threshold and limits of this review

The manuscript can become a defensible research paper by repairing B1 and implementing the main argumentative revisions I1–I4. The desired outcome is not a longer catalogue of caveats. It is a clear positive thesis: specified representations can support exact attributions about the same bearer only under the corresponding preservation and realization conditions; distinct grounds of purpose remain distinct even when their consequences coexist in one source.

I would reassess a revision by asking four questions:

1. Does the paper say exactly what ground makes its positive example purposive, and distinguish that from demographic performance?
2. Does it meet Corti's biological-normativity objection at its actual level, either by argument or by explicitly withholding the stronger conclusion?
3. Can a reader explain what Bich and Cuciniello would accept, reject, or leave open in the case, and what the preservation analysis adds?
4. Is the necessity claim restricted to the intended exact attribution, and is the common realization more than a juxtaposition of independently possible properties?

This review did not rerun Lean, validate the underlying mathematical books independently, inspect a newly built PDF, or prove that no prior author has given an equivalent methodological framework. Those tasks have different evidence requirements. It did read the complete frozen manuscript, verify the word and reference-key counts, and compare its philosophical attributions with the examined original literature.

**Final review judgment:** a worthwhile and unusually auditable reconstruction with a genuine organizing example; **major revision required**, chiefly to make its positive purposive conclusion and closest-literature comparison as careful as its mathematical limitations already are.
