This clarifies the contribution of the implementation detail. The manuscript also retains the fact that different named programs have their own stored codes and their own damaged founders.

### Exact, probabilistic, and aggregate attribution are separated

Lines 17 and 99–101 improve the preservation condition. Exact reconstruction of an individual's paid fee is distinguished from a distribution or expectation supported by supplementary evidence. The equal-probability illustration makes clear that a representation need not preserve every realized detail to support a useful aggregate explanation.

This prevents the condition from excluding ordinary scientific abstraction. The condition is applied to the actual target and its claimed precision, rather than to an indiscriminate requirement that every explanation preserve the entire underlying history.

Line 305 now treats the elementary factorization claim as analytic. Relevance judgments, proposed realization, realist interpretation, and purpose attribution remain contestable.
