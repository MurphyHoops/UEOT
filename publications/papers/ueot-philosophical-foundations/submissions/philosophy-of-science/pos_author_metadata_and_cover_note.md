- **Abstract (98 words — strictly $\le$ 100 words):**
  > When does predictive adequacy justify ontological claims about what persists or teleological claims about what an activity is for? We argue that forward-marginalized prediction systematically erases the counterfactual invariance, restorative expenditure, and genealogical contrasts grounding objecthood and purpose. Using measure-theoretic and control separations alongside a stochastic model of damage, restoration, and reproduction, we show that minimal predictive quotients shift across protocols, erase repair costs across infinite birth horizons, lose gate-mediated agency, and separate finite-memory viability from reactive control. Proliferative investment splits lineage growth from individual survival, motivating a three-criterion relational realism and a four-rung bearer-indexed account of purpose.
