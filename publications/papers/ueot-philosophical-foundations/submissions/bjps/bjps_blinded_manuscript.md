# Introduction: The explanatory transfer problem

Consider a physical device whose damaged active instruction register is restored by majority vote from internal backups before generating its next output, alongside an otherwise similar device never damaged. Once restored, both induce the *exact same probability law* over the entire infinite sequence of future births: recording births alone, neither larger samples nor longer horizons distinguish undamaged operation from paid restoration. Compare further enriching one-step births across the four founder states $\{\theta_0 H, \theta_0 D, \theta_1 H, \theta_1 D\}$ executing their conservative or proliferative hereditary programmes autonomously at fixed model parameters: adding a second birth census $(B_1, B_2)$ already saturates the probability-law resolution of $(B_t)_{t\ge 1}$—identifying distinct founder laws (though single runs overlap) while erasing the conservative founder's three-unit repair fee—whereas adjoining first-step cost metering $(B_1, R_1)$ separates paid from unpaid histories while leaving damaged programmes merged. Different protocol extensions thus yield *mutually incomparable refinements* of protocol laws: *which evidence channel is added determines which explanatory question becomes identifiable*. Finally, when proliferation raises expected descendants while lowering parent survival, calling it "adaptive for persistence" stays ambiguous until the bearer—parent token, re-instantiated organisation, or lineage—is specified.

These cases exemplify the *explanatory transfer problem*. Pattern realism ties ontology to non-redundant predictive compression [@dennett1987; @dennett1991; @ladymanRoss2007; @wimsatt2007]; computational mechanics and predictive state representations define minimal states from conditional future laws [@crutchfieldYoung1989; @shaliziCrutchfield2001; @littmanSuttonSingh2001; @barnettCrutchfield2015]; active inference moves from stationary Markov blankets to autonomous individuality and goal-seeking [@friston2010; @friston2013; @clark2013; @parrPezzuloFriston2022]; and inverse reinforcement learning fits scalar rewards to behaviour [@puterman1994; @ngRussell2000].

Selective representation is indispensable; trouble begins when predictive adequacy on one channel is transferred to claims about mechanism, persistence, or purpose depending on quotiented distinctions. Critiques of blanket metaphysics [@raja2021; @bruineberg2022] and organisational normativity [@corti2023; @bich2024; @cuciniello2026] expose fragments of this gap, yet lack a unified formal diagnosis paired with constructive compatibility conditions.

Underlying our results is an explicit *forward-marginalisation condition*: when distinct histories enter the same subsequent state law and neither sunk fees, transient repair traces, nor carry-over balances enter downstream kernels or outputs, the entire future output law is history-invariant (whereas carry-over depletion would leave downstream traces). Governed by a typed *principle of explanatory preservation*, we defend three theses (self-contained proofs in Appendix A, expanded derivations in the Online Technical Appendix, and standard-library checks in the bundled triple-anonymised `verify_standalone.py` alongside `generate_figures.py`):

1. **Constrained relational objecthood.** Three checks—*(i) predictive–control compression*, *(ii) counterfactual intervention invariance* [@hacking1983; @woodward2003; @craver2007], and *(iii) realisation-cost accountability* [@jonas1966; @rosen1991; @montevilMossio2015]—exclude individuation from passive output blankets alone while leaving symmetric fission underdetermined.
2. **Bearer-indexed grounds of purpose.** Purposive claims are indexed to bearer, contrast class, mechanism, and ground across four levels: *(Rung 1) systemic role* [@cummins1975], *(Rung 2) conditional regulatory and repair standards* [@rosenblueth1943; @mossio2009; @diPaolo2005; @bich2024], *(Rung 3) retrospective selective retention or reproduction* [@wright1973; @millikan1984; @godfreySmith1994; @garson2019]—separated from prospective growth—and *(Rung 4) practical reason* [@korsgaard1996; @scanlon1998; @street2006].
3. **Benchmarking autonomous programmes against full-policy ceilings.** Within regenerative model $\mathcal{M}$, we benchmark *autonomous hereditary execution* against the *full public-history growth ceiling* $\rho_* = \max\{1, \lambda_0, \lambda_1\}$, separating hereditary persistence from plastic dormancy switching.

Section 2 establishes predictive minimality, belief-quotient descent, and relational objecthood; Section 3 constructs $\mathcal{M}$ and proves incomparable refinements and gate separation; Section 4 analyses persistence regimes, finite-memory viability, and fission; Section 5 proves evaluative underdetermination, Theorem 1, and four-rung stratification; Section 6 diagnoses overreaches, answers four objections, and specifies empirical tests; Section 7 concludes.

# Predictive equivalence, sufficient quotients, and relational objecthood

## Protocol-indexed predictive minimality and its limits

Let $(\mathcal{H}, \Sigma_{\mathcal{H}}, \mu)$ be a probability space of realised histories and $\mathcal{I}$ a countable index set of observation or open-loop protocols, each assigning to $h \in \mathcal{H}$ a Markov kernel $K_i(h, \cdot)$ on a countably generated $(\mathcal{Y}_i, \Sigma_{\mathcal{Y}_i})$ [@blackwell1953; @kallenberg2021]. The *canonical predictive representation* for $\mathcal{P} = \{K_i\}_{i \in \mathcal{I}}$ is $C_{\mathcal{P}}(h) := (K_i(h, \cdot))_{i \in \mathcal{I}}$, inducing $h \sim_{\mathcal{P}} h' \iff C_{\mathcal{P}}(h) = C_{\mathcal{P}}(h')$.

**Proposition 1 (Countable-protocol predictive minimality).** *Suppose $\mathcal{I}$ is countable and each $(\mathcal{Y}_i, \Sigma_{\mathcal{Y}_i})$ is countably generated. If $q: (\mathcal{H}, \Sigma_{\mathcal{H}}) \to (\mathcal{Z}, \Sigma_{\mathcal{Z}})$ is predictively sufficient for $\mathcal{P}$ on $(\mathcal{H}, \Sigma_{\mathcal{H}}, \mu)$—so for every $i \in \mathcal{I}$ there exists a measurable kernel $\widetilde{K}_i$ with $K_i(h, \cdot) = \widetilde{K}_i(q(h), \cdot)$ $\mu$-almost everywhere—then there exists a measurable decoder $\psi: \mathcal{Z} \to \prod_{i \in \mathcal{I}} \Delta(\mathcal{Y}_i)$ such that $C_{\mathcal{P}}(h) = \psi(q(h))$ for $\mu$-almost every $h \in \mathcal{H}$.*

Proposition 1 generalises causal-state minimality [@crutchfieldYoung1989; @shaliziCrutchfield2001], predictive state representations [@littmanSuttonSingh2001], and $\varepsilon$-transducers [@barnettCrutchfield2015]. Three boundaries govern explanatory transfer: countability of $\mathcal{I}$ and standard Borel spaces underwrite almost-everywhere factorisation and recursive filtering [@astrom1965; @kaelbling1998]; passive input conditioning matches $\mathrm{do}(A=a)$ only under non-confounding, non-anticipatory action semantics [@pearl2009; @woodward2003]; and *predictive classes are response types, not enduring tokens* [@wiggins2001].

## Typed explanatory preservation and quotient descent

Exact attribution of $F: \mathcal{S} \to \mathcal{V}$ from $q: \mathcal{S} \to \mathcal{Z}$ requires $F = \bar{F} \circ q$ (fibre-constancy). Our *Typed Principle of Explanatory Preservation* specifies $\langle \mathcal{B},\, F,\, \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{prec}},\, \mathcal{I}\rangle$ across four tiers: *(1) token attribution*, requiring fibre-constancy on $(q(h), E_{\mathrm{aux}}(h))$; *(2) distributional attribution*, where a justified fibre prior $(1/2, 1/2)$ over costs $\{0,3\}$ preserves mean cost $3/2$ without identifying individual runs; *(3) closed-loop belief descent* [@burkeRosenblatt1958; @kemenySnell1960; @poupartBoutilier2003; @givanDeanGreig2003]; and *(4) counterfactual transport* across every $P^{\mathrm{do}(\iota)}$ ($\iota \in \mathcal{I}$) [@woodward2000; @rubenstein2017].

**Proposition 2 (Exact belief-quotient descent via closed test spaces).** *Let $(\mathcal{X}, \mathcal{A}, \mathcal{O}, T, O)$ be a finite partially observed Markov control model, and let $\mathcal{F} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{\mathcal{X}}$ be a linear subspace with $\mathbf{1} \in \mathcal{F}$ inducing $b \equiv_{\mathcal{F}} b' \iff \forall f \in \mathcal{F},\ \langle b, f\rangle = \langle b', f\rangle$. Define $(U_{a,o} f)(x) := \sum_{x'} T(x' \mid x, a) O(o \mid a, x') f(x')$. Then $\Delta(\mathcal{X})/{\equiv_{\mathcal{F}}}$ jointly preserves (i) one-step observation probabilities $p(o \mid b, a)$ and (ii) normalised posterior equivalence $\mathrm{Bayes}(b,a,o) \equiv_{\mathcal{F}} \mathrm{Bayes}(b',a,o)$ across all positive-probability branches $(a,o)$ if and only if $U_{a,o}\mathcal{F} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ for all $(a,o)$.*

Proposition 2 adapts invariant-subspace belief compression [@poupartBoutilier2003]: sufficiency holds on any $\equiv_{\mathcal{F}}$-saturated set, whereas necessity uses interior perturbations on $\Delta(\mathcal{X})$. On a forward-update-closed reachable set $\mathcal{R}$ from a fixed prior, descent requires matching observation laws and posterior $\mathcal{F}$-expectations only across *reachable pairs $(b,b') \in \mathcal{R}^2$ merged in one $\equiv_{\mathcal{F}}$-fibre*, not invariance on $\operatorname{span}(\mathcal{R})$ (under deterministic cycle $0 \to 1 \to 2 \to 0$ with trivial observations from $\delta_0$, $\mathcal{R} = \{\delta_0,\delta_1,\delta_2\}$ spans $\mathbb{R}^3$, yet $\mathcal{F} = \operatorname{span}\{\mathbf{1}, (0,1,2)\}$ separates $\mathcal{R}$ injectively while $U(0,1,2) = (1,2,0) \notin \mathcal{F}$). Observational closure $U_{a,o}\mathcal{F} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ preserves neither sunk repair costs (Tier 1) nor surgical interventions (Tier 4).

## Against pure compressionism and blanket metaphysics

Constructive empiricists restrict warrant to observable adequacy [@vanFraassen1980], whereas structural realists defend relations preserved across theory change [@worrall1989; @ladyman1998]. Dennett's [-@dennett1991] *Real Patterns* and Ladyman and Ross's [-@ladymanRoss2007] *Rainforest Realism* tie objecthood to projectible compression, while free-energy theorists treat stationary *Markov blankets* as individuating self-evidencing agents [@friston2013; @parrPezzuloFriston2022].

While rightly rejecting Lockean essences [@whitehead1929; @dupre1993; @dupreNicholson2018], passive compression underdetermines persistence: epistemic *Pearl blankets* arise in passive diffusions lacking cohesion [@raja2021; @bruineberg2022; @biehl2021], and output compression erases paid repair (Proposition 3). Pattern realists [@dennett1991; @ladymanRoss2007] can add interventional and calorimetric channels; our contribution specifies *which added channel licenses which claim*. Under *Constrained Relational Objecthood*, *(1) multi-channel predictive–control compression* [@wimsatt2007], *(2) counterfactual intervention invariance* [@hacking1983; @woodward2003; @craver2007], and *(3) realisation-cost accountability* [@jonas1966; @rosen1991; @montevilMossio2015] exclude passive-blanket individuation [@cartwright1999], while symmetrical fission (Section 4.3) marks where index continuation stays underdetermined.

# A unified stochastic regenerative model of repair, execution, and reproduction

## Physical architecture, internal programmes, and resource accounting

We construct a finite stochastic regenerative process $\mathcal{M}$ (Table 1; Figure 1a; Appendix A.3). At census $t \in \mathbb{N}$, each living token has: *(i) two three-bit rows* $(r_H, r_D)$—one *active* and two *backup* cells per row—encoding `care` ($0$) or `bloom` ($1$); *(ii) a physical gate* $g \in \{\mathrm{open}, \mathrm{closed}\}$ required for raw actuation; *(iii) a bootstrap voter* $b_{\mathrm{boot}} \in \{0,1\}$; *(iv) a six-unit budget* ($1$ carried escrow plus $5$ inflow units: repair $3$, `care` $1$, `bloom` $2$, plus $2$ escrowed for up to two outputs); and *(v) genealogical identity* (retention vs. strict birth) plus a silent tag $\tau$.

Compare hereditary programmes $\theta_0 = (\mathrm{care}, \mathrm{care})$ and $\theta_1 = (\mathrm{bloom}, \mathrm{care})$. Standard damage flips both active bits while preserving backups and $b_{\mathrm{boot}}=1$, closing $g$; each token's internal gate/parity diagnosis $d \in \{H, D\}$ is read without external oracles and exposed in the public interface readable by $\Pi_{\mathrm{pub}}$. Damaged `care` spends $3$ on majority restoration, reopens $g$, and spends $1+2=3$ on `care` ($3+3=6$). Damaged `bloom` requires $3+2+2=7 > 6$: hardware *rejects* it, refunds inflow, and retains the damaged token w.p. $1$. Executed steps copy restored rows before independent post-step active-cell damage strikes each output w.p. $q$; `stop` terminates with zero outputs.

| Healthy `bloom` ($H, \mathrm{bloom}$) | Retains parent w.p. $s - \delta$; 1 strict newborn w.p. $b + \gamma$ | Repair $0 +$ action $2 +$ escrow $2 \le 6$ |

## Task-relative predictive partitions and incomparable refinements

Fix any strict parameter vector in Table 1 and let the four single-founder states $\theta_0 H$, $\theta_0 D$, $\theta_1 H$, and $\theta_1 D$ execute their hereditary programmes autonomously. Whereas policies in $\Pi_{\mathrm{pub}}$ read initial $d_0 \in \{H, D\}$ (fixing autonomous $R_1$ directly), demographic output protocols record only strict births $B_t$ unless adjoined with initial repair cost $R_1 \in \{0,3\}$.

**Proposition 3 (Two–three–four predictive separation, fixed-parameter two-step saturation, and incomparable refinements).** *At any fixed strict parameter vector in Table 1, under autonomous hereditary execution from $\{\theta_0 H, \theta_0 D, \theta_1 H, \theta_1 D\}$: (1) predicting $B_1$ yields **two** minimal law classes, $\{\theta_0 H, \theta_0 D, \theta_1 D\}$ and $\{\theta_1 H\}$; (2) adding a **second** birth census $(B_1, B_2)$ already saturates the probability-law resolution of $(B_t)_{t\ge 1}$, yielding **three** law classes, $\{\theta_0 H, \theta_0 D\}$, $\{\theta_1 D\}$, and $\{\theta_1 H\}$, coupling $\theta_0 H$ and $\theta_0 D$ identically for all $t \ge 1$ while separating $\theta_0 D$ from $\theta_1 D$ at $t=2$ ($\mathbb{E}_{\theta_1 D}[B_2] - \mathbb{E}_{\theta_0 D}[B_2] = (s+b)(1-q)\gamma > 0$; $27/64$ vs. $39/64$ at $(3/4,1/8,3/8,1/4,1/3)$); (3) predicting $(B_1, R_1)$ refines $(B_1)$ to an incomparable **three**-class partition $\{\theta_0 H\}, \{\theta_1 H\}, \{\theta_0 D, \theta_1 D\}$, while $(B_1, B_2, R_1)$ yields **four** singleton probability laws.*

## Lawful tag quotients versus von Neumann's syntactic–constructor gap

**Proposition 4 (Silent-tag lumpability versus decoded-sequence gate separation).** *(a) Erasing silent tag $\tau$ defines a strong Markov lumpability [@burkeRosenblatt1958] commuting with transitions, six-unit budgets, repair costs, genealogy, and expectations across all $\tau$-blind policies lifted from the quotient (in particular, across $\Pi_{\mathrm{pub}}$). # Persistence, paid repair, and diachronic bearers

## Dynamical stability, servocontrol, paid repair, and reciprocal closure

Universal loop or density signatures [@friston2013] conflate four regimes (Table 3). First, *passive invariance* ($\dot{x} = -x$ on $\mathbb{R}^n$, $H_1(\mathbb{R}^n) = 0$) yields compact $\omega(x_0) = \{0\}$ without cycles or repair. Second, *external servocontrol* [@rosenblueth1943; @conantAshby1970] regulates state via fixed hardware it never regenerates. Third, *paid endogenous repair and constraint closure* [@kant1790, §§64–65; @varela1974; @maturanaVarela1980; @weberVarela2002; @rosen1991; @kauffman1993; @deacon2011; @mossio2009; @montevilMossio2015] spend work to regenerate degrading constraints [@jonas1966; @thompson2007]. In $\mathcal{M}$, active rows and $g$ degrade at rate $q$ on $\tau_{\mathrm{step}}=1$ and are rebuilt via $b_{\mathrm{boot}}=1$ held fixed on $\tau_{\mathrm{boot}} \gg 1$; full Montévil–Mossio closure adds a slow loop regenerating $b_{\mathrm{boot}}$ and backups. Fourth, *adaptive viability* [@aubinCellina1984; @diPaolo2005; @barandiaran2009; @bich2024] modulates modes near viability boundaries.

## Minimal cut-sets, finite-memory viability, and fixed-sample limits

Under monotone deletion failure on downward-closed finite $\mathcal{I}$, destructive perturbations are uniquely represented by the minimal cut-set antichain $\mathcal{V}_{\min}(\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{I})$ (**Proposition 5**, Appendix A.2)—isolating bootstrap severance ($b_{\mathrm{boot}}=0$), underfunding ($2 < 3$), and three-cell erasure [@coverThomas2006], while failing under disinhibition. Moreover (**Proposition 6**, Appendix A.2): *(a)* on a five-state system with maximal viable kernel $K^\star = \operatorname{gfp}(F_S) = S$ under pooled observation $h$, *every* memoryless $\pi_0: \mathcal{O} \to \Delta(\mathcal{A})$ exits $S$ in two steps w.p. $1/2$ whereas one-step memory $\pi_1: \mathcal{O}^2 \to \mathcal{A}$ maintains $\mathbb{P}_{\pi_1}(X_t \in K^\star\ \forall t \ge 0) = 1$ (Figure 2a) [@kaelbling1998; @papadimitriouTsitsiklis1987; @sethTsakiris2018]; and *(b)* for $Z_i \stackrel{\mathrm{i.i.d.}}{\sim} \mathrm{Bernoulli}(r)$, $\sup_{r \in (0,1]} \mathbb{E}_r[\psi(Z_{1:n})] \le \alpha$ forces $\mathbb{E}_0[\psi] \le \alpha$ [@leCam1973; @mayo2018].

## Symmetrical fission as a stress test of relational objecthood

Organisms persist through turnover while individuality splinters across physiological, immunological, and Darwinian criteria [@whitehead1929; @clarke2010; @godfreySmith2009; @pradeu2012; @maynardSmithSzathmary1995; @dupreNicholson2018]. Process $\mathcal{M}$ separates parental retention from strict birth despite identical post-step predictive classes [@wiggins2001]. Symmetrical fission into two equally continuous, intervention-stable, cost-accountable daughters $y_1 \neq y_2$ [@parfit1984] tests Checks (1)–(3): *both* branches satisfy all three equally, yet transitivity blocks numerical identity with both, leaving index continuation underdetermined without explicit bearer conventions. Likewise, an absorbing chain's *quasi-stationary distribution* $\nu_{\mathrm{QSD}}$ differs from the *Doob $h$-transformed* survivor law $\varpi(x) \propto \nu_{\mathrm{QSD}}(x)h(x)$ [@doob1957; @darrochSeneta1965; @collet2013; @cuciniello2026].

# Optimisation, bearer divergence, and the grounds of purpose

## Evaluative underdetermination: reward shaping, gauge, and bottleneck rank

Behavioural optimality never suffices for purpose: observed policies fit an infinite reward cone [@ngRussell2000, §3.1]; in discounted or proper-absorbing dynamic programming [@blackwell1965; @puterman1994], potential shaping $r'(x,a,x') = r(x,a,x') + \beta\psi(x') - \psi(x)$ preserves optimal policies [@ngHaradaRussell1999]; non-total multidimensional preorders lack scalar utilities [@krantz1971]; and composite scores $J = R - \lambda I$—comparable to expected free energy [@parrPezzuloFriston2022] and distinct from Tononi's [-@tononi2004] $\Phi^{\mathrm{IIT}}$—admit affine gauge $R' = R + \lambda\chi$, $I' = I + \chi$. Furthermore (**Proposition 7**, Appendix A.2), under healthy $\theta_0$ execution, $\Psi(s,b,q) := (s, b, bq)$ has $\det D\Psi = b > 0$: while not refuting scalar objectives, it rules out *losslessly factoring $(s,b,bq)$ through any smooth $(R,I) \in \mathbb{R}^2$ bottleneck*.

## Full-policy growth ceiling versus autonomous hereditary attainment

With per-token local six-unit sites, one-step output means are $k_c = s+b$ and $k_b = s-\delta+b+\gamma$, giving hereditary rates $\lambda_0 = k_c$ and $\lambda_1 = (1-q)k_b + q k_c$. Define the admissible policy class $\Pi_{\mathrm{pub}}$ explicitly: at each census $t$, $\pi \in \Pi_{\mathrm{pub}}$ reads the public history $\mathcal{F}_t$ of living counts, per-token internal/public diagnoses $d_u \in \{H, D\}$, genealogical marks, and past expenditure receipts (including $\pi_{\mathrm{dorm}}$, which plays `care` on $H$ and `bloom` on $D$), using arbitrary joint randomisation across living tokens while remaining blind to silent tags $\tau$ and contemporaneous/future actuation or damage noise.

**Theorem 1 (Full-policy growth ceiling versus autonomous hereditary attainment).** *For any strict parameters in Table 1 and finite non-empty initial population $P_0$, let $\rho_* := \max\{1, \lambda_0, \lambda_1\}$. (1) Across **all** policies $\pi \in \Pi_{\mathrm{pub}}$, there exists $C > 0$ such that $\mathbb{E}_\pi[N_t] \le C \rho_*^t$ for all $t \ge 0$, and a stationary policy in $\Pi_{\mathrm{pub}}$ attains $\Theta(\rho_*^t)$. (2) Under **autonomous multi-generational execution of the fixed hereditary programme $\theta_i$** ($i \in \{0,1\}$) from a single damaged founder $\theta_i D$, paid first-step repair ($R_1=3$), gate-mediated execution, and $\Theta(\rho_*^t)$ attainment hold jointly iff $(\gamma \le \delta \land k_c \ge 1)$ for $\theta_0$, and iff $(\gamma \ge \delta \land \lambda_1 \ge 1)$ for $\theta_1$.*

Separating the two quantifiers in Theorem 1 is essential: if a controller in $\Pi_{\mathrm{pub}}$ may *abandon* hereditary execution after $t=1$, paid repair and $\Theta(\rho_*^t)$ attainment hold even in the subcritical regime! At $(s,\delta,b,\gamma,q) = (1/2, 1/4, 1/4, 1/8, 1/3)$, $k_c = 3/4$, $k_b = 5/8$, $\lambda_1 = 2/3$, and $\rho_* = 1$: neither autonomous $\theta_0$ nor autonomous $\theta_1$ attains $\rho_*=1$, yet running `care` at $t=1$ ($R_1=3$, reopening $g$) and switching to $\pi_{\mathrm{dorm}}$ for $t \ge 2$ retains a damaged parent at $t=2$ w.p. $sq = 1/6$ (and leaves at least one $D$-token w.p. $17/72$), yielding $\Theta(1)$ via arrested dormancy rather than active lineage reproduction.

Theorem 1 (Figure 2b) yields four further lessons. First, whenever $\bar{\lambda} := \max\{\lambda_0, \lambda_1\} < 1$ (Regime I, $\rho_* = 1$), the potential $V(P_t)$ has a uniform conditional expected drop $\ge (1 - \bar{\lambda})k_c > 0$ on every actively executed `care` or `bloom` step and $0$ drop on frozen $(D,\mathrm{bloom})$ refusal ($0$ births on refusal or `stop`), telescoping to finite cumulative expected births $\mathbb{E}_\pi[\sum_{t\ge 1} B_t] \le (b+\gamma)V(P_0)/((1-\bar{\lambda})k_c) < \infty$ under *every* admissible policy $\pi \in \Pi_{\mathrm{pub}}$ (Online Appendix A.4). Attaining $\rho_* = 1$ in subcritical regimes—analogous to dormant bacterial persisters—thus preserves at most a frozen individual rather than active restorative work or continuing lineage reproduction, showing why any "maintenance" evaluation must specify its bearer first. Second, in Regime III ($\gamma > \delta, \lambda_1 \ge 1$), autonomous `bloom` maximises expected lineage growth while *reducing* parent survival ($s-\delta < s$), splitting organismal persistence from lineage multiplication [@okasha2006; @godfreySmith2009] and *differential retention* from *differential reproduction* [@garson2019]. Third, at critical $(2/3, 1/8, 1/4, 1/4, 1/3)$, $\lambda_1 = \rho_* = 1$ yet $\mathbb{E}_{\theta_1 D}[N_t] = 11/12 < 1$ for all $t \ge 1$ ($k_c=11/12, k_b=25/24$). ## Four stratified grounds of purpose and the normativity challenge

Causal-role analysis [@cummins1975] lacks malfunction norms [@davies2001], selected-effects etiology [@wright1973; @millikan1984; @neander1991; @godfreySmith1994] misses de novo regulation, and organisational accounts [@mossio2009; @bich2024] face Corti's [-@corti2023, §3] and Bedau's [-@bedau1992] challenge: why does existential dependence support a functional *ought*? Table 4 and Figure 3 stratify four non-interchangeable grounds:

|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. Systemic role** | Constitutive decomposition [@cummins1975; @craver2007] | Part in capacity $\Psi$; ablation test | Explanatory relevance; **no** malfunction norm [@davies2001] |

1. **Operational conditional standards at Rung 2—and where Corti's objection retains force.** Pointing out that biology lacks moral obligations misses @corti2023; instead, Rung 2 establishes an *operational, system-conditional standard* [@nagel1977; @walsh2015] while pinpointing where Corti's challenge still bites [@bedau1992; @cuciniello2026]. *Given* the reference standard of maintaining executable integrity in $K^\star$, $\mathcal{M}$ objectively separates *(i) malfunction* ($b_{\mathrm{boot}}=0$ or stuck $g=\mathrm{closed}$ failing to restore execution under `care`), *(ii) accidental side-effect survival* (subcritical rejected `bloom` preserving a damaged token without restoring code or $g$), and *(iii) costly normal operation* (damaged `care` paying $R_1=3$ to restore active rows and execute). 3. **Scope of Rung 4.** Process $\mathcal{M}$ contains no Rung 4 witness; Rung 4 marks the boundary showing that Rungs 1–3 never by themselves entail practical endorsement [@korsgaard1996; @street2006; @list2019].

# Competitor diagnostics, objections, and empirical tests

## Diagnosing cross-protocol leaps in contemporary programmes

*(1) Active inference:* in $\mathcal{M}$, internal rows $(r_H, r_D)$ act on external births $B_t$ locally via $(r_H, r_D) \to (d_t, g_t, a_t) \to B_t$ through diagnostic $d_t$, gate $g_t$, and executed action $a_t$ (omitting $a_t$ fails local mediation, since $\theta_0 H$ and $\theta_1 H$ share $d_1=H, g_1=\mathrm{open}$ yet yield birth rates $b$ vs. $b+\gamma$). Such local step mediation does *not* establish the stationary conditional-independence structure required by ontological Friston blankets [@friston2013; @parrPezzuloFriston2022; @bruineberg2022; @raja2021]; our specific formal addition to @bruineberg2022 is that *even an output interface sufficient to predict all future births $(B_t)_{t\ge 1}$ can erase paid endogenous repair ($R_1 = 3$ vs. $0$)* while composite scores retain affine gauge freedom [@biehl2021] and parent survival diverges from lineage growth [@sethTsakiris2018]. *(2) Computational mechanics:* minimal $\varepsilon$-transducers [@shaliziCrutchfield2001; @barnettCrutchfield2015] and macro-emergence [@hoel2013] are protocol-relative; Proposition 3 maps their cross-protocol transfer boundary. *(3) Organisational vs. evolutionary teleology:* gate $g$ captures state-sensitive modulation [@bich2024] while genealogical records govern the handoff from Rung 2 tokens to Rung 3 cohorts [@cuciniello2026].

## Constructive unification and replies to four objections

Separate models $\exists x\,R(x)$ and $\exists y\,O(y)$ do not entail $\exists z\,[R(z)\land O(z)]$ when repair consumes half the six-unit budget ($3/6$) and blocks damaged `bloom` [@friedman1974; @kitcher1989]; Theorem 1 establishes exact constrained compatibility.

1. *Constructive empiricism suffices [@vanFraassen1980].* Formal theorems never deductively compel realism; our case is comparative [@hacking1983; @woodward2003]. Specify two competing mechanisms explicitly from damaged $(\theta_0, D)$ ($g=\mathrm{closed}$): under normal $b_{\mathrm{boot}}=1$, on $(D, \mathrm{care})$ (six-unit budget $3+1+2=6$) both $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{vote}}$ (bootstrap majority-vote) and $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{bypass}}$ (an independent auxiliary `care`-latch not consulting $b_{\mathrm{boot}}$, re-engaging whenever $D$ recurs) pay $R_t = 3$ ($+1$ actuation, $+2$ escrow), restore $(r_H, r_D)$ and $g \leftarrow \mathrm{open}$ at step end, and transition into the exact same post-step law $(I_{\mathrm{ret}}, B_t) \sim \mathrm{Bern}(s)\otimes\mathrm{Bern}(b)$ of $\theta_0$-tokens (damaged w.p. $q$), yielding *identical joint laws on $((B_t, R_t))_{t\ge 1}$*. Under $\mathrm{do}(b_{\mathrm{boot}}=0)$ on $(\theta_0, D)$, $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{vote}}$ fails voting ($g$ remains $\mathrm{closed}$, $R_1=0$, recovery probability $0$, $B_t=0\ \forall t\ge 1$), whereas $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{bypass}}$ recovers w.p. $1$ via its auxiliary latch ($R_1=3$, $\mathbb{E}[B_1 \mid \mathrm{do}(b_{\mathrm{boot}}=0)] = b > 0$, preserving the full $(B_t)_{t\ge 1}$ law; @rubenstein2017). Birth laws leave undamaged $(\theta_0, H)$, $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{vote}}$, and $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{bypass}}$ tied; cost metering ($R_1=3$) rules out unpaid $(\theta_0, H)$ while leaving $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{vote}}$ and $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{bypass}}$ tied; and $\mathrm{do}(b_{\mathrm{boot}}=0)$ separates $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{vote}}$ from $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{bypass}}$ [@mayo2018].
2. *Preservation is trivially analytic.* Fibre factorisation ($F = \bar{F}\circ q$) is analytic once $F$ and $q$ are fixed; the substantive work is proving *which* compressions destroy *which* invariants—including fixed-parameter two-step birth saturation alongside infinite-horizon cost erasure (Proposition 3), sequence-versus-gate separation (Proposition 4b), finite-memory viability (Proposition 6a), and critical deficits ($11/12 < 1$).
3. *Stipulated scaffolding falls short of full closure.* Exogenous energy inflow is shared by all living cells and does not render repair fictitious [@jonas1966; @montevilMossio2015]. However, because $b_{\mathrm{boot}}=1$ is fixed on the modelled horizon, $\mathcal{M}$ establishes paid endogenous repair on the fast timescale (Table 3, 3a), leaving slow-timescale regeneration of $b_{\mathrm{boot}}$ (3b) and Rung 3 selection as explicit additional conditions.
4. *Empirical discrimination between rival mechanisms.* Causal abstraction requires exact physical probes for model variables [@craver2007; @rubenstein2017]. On an *engineered fault-tolerant register* initialised at $(\theta_0, D)$ alongside undamaged $(\theta_0, H)$, a four-arm protocol tests all three rivals directly: *(Arm 1)* output logging verifying matched $(B_t)_{t\ge 1}$ laws across $(\theta_0, H)$, $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{vote}}$, and $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{bypass}}$; *(Arm 2)* per-cycle rail metering measuring $R_1 = 3$ versus $0$; *(Arm 3)* orthogonal hardware interventions $\mathrm{do}(b_{\mathrm{boot}}=0)$ (halting $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{vote}}$ while sparing $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{bypass}}$) versus $\mathrm{do}(g=\mathrm{closed})$ post-vote (blocking raw actuation in both without corrupting backups); and *(Arm 4)* per-step budget titration testing rejection of damaged `bloom` ($7 > 6$) and parent retention versus births [@mayo2018]. *Escherichia coli* methyl-directed `MutS–MutL–MutH` mismatch repair versus translesion bypass [@auWelshModrich1992; @hasanLeach2015] provides qualitative *biological motivation* for this contrast, though bulk cellular ATP pools reflect multiple concurrent processes and cannot alone identify $R_1$.

# Conclusion

When two rival explanations share the exact same predictive law on an output channel, what additional evidence warrants accepting one over the other? Our results show that extending observations along different coordinates produces mutually incomparable refinements, that autonomous hereditary attainment of full-policy growth ceilings separates sharply from plastic dormancy switching, and that parent survival can oppose lineage multiplication. Calibrating relational objecthood and four-rung bearer-indexed purpose strictly to these evidential boundaries preserves explanatory unity without overclaiming beyond what mechanism, repair budgets, and selection history warrant.
