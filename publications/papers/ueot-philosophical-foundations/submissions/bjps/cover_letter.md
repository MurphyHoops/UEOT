# Cover Letter — Submission to *The British Journal for the Philosophy of Science*

Dear Editors of *The British Journal for the Philosophy of Science*,

I am pleased to submit **"What Prediction Leaves Out: Relational Objecthood and the Grounds of Purpose"** for consideration for publication in the *BJPS*.

The manuscript addresses a central question across philosophy of science, cognitive science, and philosophy of biology: when does a predictively sufficient representation license ontological claims about an enduring individual or teleological claims about what its activity is for? Identifying a structural *forward-marginalisation asymmetry*, the paper proves four formal non-reductions and one joint-realisability theorem within a self-contained stochastic model of damage, restoration, and reproduction, defending a three-criterion account of constrained relational objecthood and a four-rung bearer-indexed stratification of purposive grounds.
