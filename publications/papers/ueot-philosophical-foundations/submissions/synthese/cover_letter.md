# Cover Letter — Submission to *Synthese*

Dear Editors-in-Chief of *Synthese*,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, **"What Prediction Leaves Out: Relational Objecthood and the Grounds of Purpose,"** for publication as an original research article in *Synthese*.

Across contemporary philosophy of cognitive science, theoretical biology, and formal epistemology—spanning predictive processing, active inference and Markov-blanket ontologies, computational mechanics, and pattern realism—predictive compression and statistical optimality are widely treated as licensing conclusions about what persists as an individual and what its activity is for. Combining measure-theoretic and partially observed control separations with a unified stochastic model of damage, majority-vote restoration, and reproduction (with all mathematical proofs self-contained in the Appendix), this article establishes four strict non-reductions and one constructive compatibility theorem, developing a three-criterion account of *constrained relational objecthood* and a four-rung *bearer-indexed stratification of purposive grounds*.
