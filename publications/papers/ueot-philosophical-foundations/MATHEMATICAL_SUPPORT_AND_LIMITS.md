## 1. Verdict for the author

The mathematics gives the philosophical paper a substantial discipline of **separate explanatory obligations**, with explicit positive connections in a finite source model. It does not prove a metaphysics of natural objects. Three results are particularly useful:

1. A canonical predictive representation is minimal relative to specified response kernels, protocols, future records and a reference law. Its minimality is an exact result; the choice of these inputs and the inference from a successful representation to real organization remain separate responsibilities.
2. Prediction, implementation, repair, action permission and historical identity cannot safely be collapsed into one relation. The regenerative source supplies actual counterexamples to such collapse and an explicit lawful quotient preserving the relevant joint records.
3. Once an independently specified survival/birth mechanism, observation interface, transaction rule and resource regime are fixed, one can derive a behavioral ranking and compare it with implemented hereditary programs. The latest result identifies exact parameter regions of growth attainment. This is considerably stronger than assigning a favorable number to an observed action after the fact, but it remains conditional on the specified mechanism and population evaluation.

The paper may defend a world-constrained relational realism as a **philosophical interpretation supported and restricted by these distinctions**. It must not describe that interpretation as a theorem of Lean. Likewise, a mechanism can ground a contextual goal explanation without deciding that growth is the organism's only purpose, its intention, or a morally justified end.

## 3. Predictive identity: the exact support for relational objecthood

The basic state is a response array, \(C(h)=(K_i(h,\cdot))_i\). In `PredictionDependent.canonical_ae_minimal`, the protocol index is countable and every supplied response kernel factors almost everywhere through a competing statistic via a measurable kernel. The conclusion is a measurable decoder from that statistic to the entire response array, on one common almost-everywhere set. The proof uses the countable interchange of almost-everywhere quantifiers and the measurable product decoder.

There is a useful subtlety: the Lean endpoint needs only the measurable structures **once the kernels are supplied**; Standard Borel assumptions in the mathematical account justify the construction and good behavior of the required conditional laws. The paper should not conflate these two layers or claim a universal well-behaved measurable quotient of arbitrary observations.

The later `MeasurablePredictiveCanonicalSource` strengthens the source connection. It constructs an actual observed-past/future joint law from the same latent transition and observation kernels, retains the terminal latent source rather than redrawing it, and derives the conditional-future factorization. `actual_response_ae_minimal` then consumes the stable minimality theorem. The reviewed endpoint concerns all finite open-loop action words following the generated past; its broader adaptive successors have their own contracts and are not silently imported into this statement. `actual_canonical_recursion` is almost everywhere on the actual next-observation law and requires the appropriate Standard Borel observation condition.

**Permitted interpretation:** protocol-relative identification can be mathematically objective once its inputs are fixed: two histories either do or do not induce the same declared response laws. Finer tasks can require finer states. This gives a constrained, corrigible notion of identification.

**Not established:** that the response class is the whole intrinsic nature of a physical bearer; that prediction alone establishes intervention validity; that one task yields the one true inventory of objects; or that a predictive equivalence class determines unique material location, ancestry, execution conditions or value.

The most useful current book example is the fixed-program four-initial-state classification. For \(\theta_0H,\theta_0D,\theta_1H,\theta_1D\), one-step strict-birth prediction has two classes; the joint strict-birth record over every finite horizon has three; adding the first repair fee, or preserving full public observation, gives four. The proof couples the first program's healthy and damaged starts after their different initial payment, and separates the damaged programs at the second birth count. Crucially, this complete 2/3/4 classification is **written mathematics with independent exact verification, not a newly established dedicated Lean theorem**. It is also a fixed-internal-program prediction task, not the full external-control quotient.

## 4. Persistence and Ω: what must be revised

`PersistenceOmega.p_per_01` gives a nonempty compact invariant omega-limit set from a continuous nonnegative-time semiflow, a precompact orbit and eventual residence in a closed domain. It does not give attraction of a neighborhood, a geometric loop, nonzero first homology, repair, or a selected goal. The familiar example \(\dot x=-x\) on a contractible state space separates stability from nontrivial first homology; it is a written counterexample in mathematics §9.5, not a newly audited dedicated homology endpoint here.

`OmegaMinimalFailure.p_omg_01` is exact reconstruction of a monotone finite-deletion failure family from its minimal members. Formally, the ambient mechanism type may be arbitrary because each deletion set is finite; the source's finite-mechanism setting is a sufficient special case. The reverse implication depends on monotonicity. If removing an inhibitor enables a backup, that assumption may fail. A frozen certificate and intervention semantics precede the construction.

Two no-go claims must stay distinct:

- A geometric cycle or nontrivial \(H_1\) is not necessary for persistence.
- A nonempty destructive family is not guaranteed for **every declared intervention family**. The definitive book's nonconstant readout \(q(x,u)=x\) is untouched by cuts restricted to \(u\). This is a useful written example of protocol-relative causal integrity; it does not show that no physically possible intervention could damage the system.

The carrier/blocker results are about consistent readouts and access erasure. Turning a loss of access into physical destruction requires the separate frame conditions. A readout may cease to be available while the process continues; conversely, a physical intervention can change dynamics rather than merely remove a coordinate.

## 5. The strongest finite positive example

The regenerative source is an explicitly defined finite model, not a biological or hardware experiment. Within that model, the source is substantive: success, actual first moments, hereditary transfer and optimality are computed from the same program and event definitions instead of being assumed as successful interfaces.

There are two three-bit rows, a main execution gate, a bootstrap capability and a causally silent tag. Damage flips each active bit and closes the main gate while preserving the two backups in each row. Majority repair restores the code without consulting an external correct-program label. `actual_program_reads_restored_code` proves that the restored **actual active cell** is read. `step_budget` preserves the complete six-unit event account. Damage repair costs three, leaving three for care and the reserve of two possible outputs. Outputs copy the restored physical code before the specified damage; faithful decoding follows from those operations.

This is not unrestricted self-production. The bootstrap and write mechanism are trusted primitives, backup information survives the specified damage, each existing body receives five new resource units per census, and subsequent survival/birth noise can still kill it. `bootstrap_cut_not_repairable`, `underfunded_not_repairable` and `no_decoder_recovers_both_erased` make these limitations mathematically visible. The funding no-go fixes the repair-stage balance and does not rule out later rescue by new supply.

The same-source repair-and-growth theorem binds the same damaged founder, actual code and six-unit account. It avoids the invalid strategy of combining one system's repair theorem with a different system's growth theorem. It thereby supports a modest compatibility claim: in a nonempty family of models, genuinely implemented local regeneration and population-growth attainment can coexist under one consistent mechanism. It does not establish that all persisting objects regenerate or that all natural agents optimize growth.

## 6. Latest parameter classification: exact meaning and an informative artifact

The currently reviewed positive probability domain is

\[
0<\delta<s<1,\qquad b>0,\quad\gamma>0,\quad b+\gamma<1,\qquad0<q<1.
\]

The two action output rates are \(k_c=s+b\), \(k_b=s-\delta+b+\gamma\), and the two hereditary program factors are

\[
\lambda_0=k_c,\qquad \lambda_1=(1-q)k_b+qk_c.
\]

The full public-history policy class permits real randomization and correlated requests across current members. It sees the declared observations and public receipts, not current world noise or hidden audit fields. The original canonical state invariant and transaction semantics remain in force. The least positive uniform exponential upper factor is

\[
\rho_*=\max\{1,\lambda_0,\lambda_1\}.
\]

One constant bounds \(E N_n\le C\rho_*^n\) for **all** allowed policies and all finite \(n\); an explicit stationary policy has positive upper and lower constant multiples of \(\rho_*^n\). Every competitor is not assumed to possess a growth limit. The exact two-program attainment regions, including ties, are:

| Program | Necessary and sufficient attainment condition |
|---|---|
| \(\theta_0=(\mathrm{care},\mathrm{care})\) | \(\gamma\le\delta\) and \(k_c\ge1\) |
| \(\theta_1=(\mathrm{bloom},\mathrm{care})\) | \(\gamma\ge\delta\) and \(\lambda_1\ge1\) |

`same_damaged_program_repairs_and_attains_iff` binds each program to its **own** damaged founder and paid restoration. A `programPolicy` on an arbitrary canonical population is first a public request rule. Its interpretation as inherited internal execution needs the `CarriesProgram` invariant and `programPolicy_positive_branch_is_internal`; those premises hold for the named founder applications.

The source explains the extra \(1\). A damaged body's bloom request cannot be prepaid; rejection preserves that body, refunds the inflow, and does not subject it to this transaction's noise. Repeating rejection yields an identity row. This is a conspicuous **model assumption**, not a theorem that natural systems avoid decay by refusing activity. It makes the result a good case of why causal semantics and action permissions matter, but a poor basis for any universal natural-law claim.

Exponential attainment differs from finite-deadline population maximization. The repository gives exact counterexamples. In the old positive class, bloom also reduces the same healthy individual's one-step retention probability from \(s\) to \(s-\delta\), while favoring population growth. Thus survival of an individual, number of successors, growth factor, lineage survival and normative good must not be treated as interchangeable meanings of “persistence.” Positive-root limits and limsup interpretations are written consequences of the finite bounds; no dedicated Lean root-limit endpoint is claimed in the latest contract.

## 7. Selection and historical identity

The new cohort feedback has actual inherited program counts and independent source noise. For fixed finite horizon \(T\), independent cohorts bound the discrepancy between a realized type ratio and its ratio of expected counts. Extinction remains in the failure event. The final existence theorem chooses a horizon first and then a finite number of separately funded cohorts. It is not a theorem of almost-sure selection in one fixed finite population.

At the specified rational source, the higher-growth program vanishes in the first census while the lower-growth program remains nonzero with probability \(243/2048\). The expected high-program share with zero-population share set to zero is \(2095/4096\); conditioning on nonzero population gives \(2095/4006\); the ratio of expected counts is \(10/19\). These exact formal calculations decisively prevent exchanging expectation and normalization. The fate of the surviving lower-growth family at infinite time is not settled by this event.

The actual genealogy separately marks retained identities and strict newborns. The many-to-one theorem weights descendants by reproductive value; its Doob-type lineage kernel is a statistical weighting of actual marked histories. It is not uniform sampling among living individuals and is not automatically executable by the original controller. In particular, a stochastic kernel's row sums equaling one do not establish permitted-action realizability.

These results support historical and causal constraints on object identification. They do not make ancestry recoverable from every predictive quotient. The lawful tag-erasure quotient preserves actual transaction and history laws; deleting a damage diagnostic destroys an affordability distinction, and preserving only decoded code loses gate-dependent execution. The latter is a particularly clear illustration for a philosophy readership.

## 8. Purpose, optimization and Π/Φ

The stable N-TEL-01 argument fixes a dynamics and feasible actions and gives opposite rewards to two different first actions. Therefore those physical-control facts alone do not select a unique evaluation. The explicit Lean `PurposeIdentifiability` product example has an even narrower scope: it attaches different numerical objectives to the same bare evidence value. The file expressly says it does **not** prove the existence of two physically admissible full models with identical complete evidence but different mechanism-grounded purposes. Do not upgrade that product construction into an impossibility theorem for naturalized function.

The source-derived regenerative ranking is compatible with that no-go. Additional facts about survival, birth, resource constraints and the selected evaluation determine a ranking in a particular class. Proving that a strategy would maximize that ranking is separate from proving that a physical system executes it, and both are separate from the evidence that differential inheritance changes population composition.

GOD is first a correspondence of optimal actions. A chosen greedy representative does not make it unique. The `RegisterFaultDynamicPrice.threshold_tie` endpoint gives a real tie between the compiled repair controller and idle at the explicit price/discount/repair threshold. GOA is also typed: invariant law, Cesàro limit, killed conditional law, deterministic limit set and optimal-policy long-run family are distinct. `FiniteCesaroInvariant.p_goa_01` supplies subsequences and invariance, not uniqueness or full mixing. A further four-state source gives the complete family of invariant mixtures for its safety-restricted closed loop.

The Π/Φ gauge identity is algebraic: \((\Pi+\lambda\chi)-\lambda(\Phi+\chi)=\Pi-\lambda\Phi\). It prevents identifying the separate axes from that combined value alone; it does not itself supply the right physical measurements or establish that two axes suffice. The common two-dimensional factorization theorem proves a necessary Jacobian rank bound at one fixed budget point, with the **same** differentiable latent map across environments. Per-environment rank two, stacking derivatives from different points, or failing to reject a noisy third singular value cannot be substituted for this condition. A real-valued representation of all general preferences is not automatic either: `incomparableBoolContract_has_no_numerical_representation` formalizes the incomparability obstruction.

## 9. What is scientifically unverified

The definitive Core status is `CLASSIFIED_CORE_CLOSED` with empirical status `UNVERIFIED`. This means declared positive claims have conditional proofs, stronger claims have explicit boundaries or withdrawals, and interpretive claims retain philosophical responsibility. It does not mean that the mechanisms of cells, minds or societies have been experimentally shown to instantiate the model.

The current physics README and independently read `physics/qm/status/CURRENT.json` report v31.9 / Kernel 1.9 as `LOCAL_VERIFIED_REMOTE_PENDING`. The JSON records local machine and book acceptance as `PASS`, integration acceptance as `PENDING`, current source/PR/main-CI identity fields as null, and full natural theory and new experimental confirmation as `NOT_CLAIMED`. Historical v31.8 remote verification must not be substituted for a v31.9 run. The physics book itself distinguishes definitions, conditional derivations, extra physical principles, interpretive connections and experiments. It expressly does not derive unique quantum axioms from generic Core, complete four-dimensional interacting QFT, or quantum gravity. The new philosophy article should use physics for the lesson that shared interfaces do not remove domain-specific premises, not as evidence of a completed cosmological unification. This audit reuses the exact status disclosure; it did not rerun or independently certify the physics proof suite.

## 11. Suggested economy of the formal content

For an 8,000–12,000-word philosophy paper, the main text needs at most three short formal displays: the response equivalence/minimality schema, the evaluative non-entailment/gauge example, and the source-specific growth factor with its explicit domain. Most of the 106-result catalog should stay in supporting material. The most philosophically effective counterexample is that preserving decoded code need not preserve execution; the most informative positive witness is same-source paid recovery with conditional growth attainment. Their role is to make a focused argument about legitimate and illegitimate explanatory transfer, not to display the project's theorem inventory.
