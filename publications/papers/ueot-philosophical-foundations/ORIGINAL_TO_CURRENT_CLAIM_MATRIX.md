## The original argument as a connected proposal

The original manuscript's central concern is not the notation itself. It attempts to place matter, life, mind, and meaning in one natural world. Its explanatory chain is: stable existence requires self-maintaining organization; organization has a closed-loop topology; change follows physical and informational improvement; a locally directed flow leads toward a unique optimal attractor; function and purpose are therefore natural rather than supernatural; and human meaning, freedom, and obligation are higher-level expressions of this same direction. The ambition is visible in O 2–3 and 40–41; T 40–64 and 725–760.

The first and last philosophical motivations survive substantial mathematical criticism. The middle inferential steps do not survive unchanged. The current book explicitly describes its revisions as new collaborative work rather than attributing them retrospectively to the original author (P 35). An honest paper should preserve both facts: the original asked a consequential philosophical question, and it made strong commitments that the reconstructed account now withdraws.

## Accuracy constraints for the new manuscript

1. **Preserve the historical discontinuity.** Original Ω really did invoke homology and full state return; later Ω is a reconstruction. Original openness was already dynamic. Both facts matter.
2. **Do not silently equate three identities.** Task equivalence, causal implementation, and historical individual continuity can overlap but need distinct evidence.
3. **State the 2/3/4 scope exactly.** Fixed internal programs and four initial states; `B_t` is strict newborn count in the entire actual population at tick `t`. It is not total population size, and it is not a one-body observation after later branching. Four classes follow from the whole finite-future birth task plus `R₁`; the smaller task `(B₁,R₁)` still merges the two damaged programs (M 124, 148–150).
4. **Separate functional direction from an internal goal.** Counts and rankings can be generated by a real mechanism even when the reason to privilege that count is supplied by the question. Feedback control toward a goal and an agent's endorsement of that goal are additional explanations.
5. **Respect the newest growth classification.** The selected bloom program is not universally optimal. Its attainment region, the care program's region, ties, and refusal region all exist. The critical finite-horizon figure is **11/12**, not 12/11.
6. **Do not turn non-attainment of two fixed programs into impossibility of regenerative optimal control.** A one-time paid repair prefix can remain compatible with exponent 1 (M 282).
7. **Do not flatten evidence status.** The complete task partition, the three-response obstruction, and special horizon examples are written proofs and exact calculations. The growth classification has Lean contracts for finite-horizon bounds, minimal base, and attainment iff; source notes explicitly distinguish their written limit interpretation. Empirical applicability is unverified.
8. **Let the positive account do work.** The source actually makes damage, stored information, repair cost, execution, retained parent, new child, and subsequent action part of one mechanism. Its philosophical contribution is not that every ambitious claim failed, but that this connected explanation can survive the failures without manufacturing an ultimate purpose or a moral law.

## Recommended central claim

The most defensible concentrated thesis is this: **a persistent organization can be a real object and the bearer of limited functional direction even when no single predictive quotient completely individuates it and no single optimization criterion exhausts its purposes.** The same physical history supports objective but task-relative descriptions; realization and continuity explain what those descriptions track; the embodied effects of activity provide nonarbitrary functional comparisons; and further evidence is required to identify endogenous goals, reasons, or obligations.

This is a proposed philosophical conclusion, not a theorem already delivered by the source. Its strongest support is the combination of the paid-repair realization, the 2/3/4 task partitions, and the contrast between parent retention and lineage growth. Its strongest vulnerability is novelty: the final manuscript must show exactly what this combination adds to established process, organizational, and teleological accounts rather than claiming a new ontology because the implementation is formally detailed.
