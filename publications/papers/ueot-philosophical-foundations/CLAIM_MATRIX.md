## Novelty, borrowing, and unresolved questions

The elementary fibre argument, predictive causal states, process persistence, organizational functions, regulatory teleology, and the separation of fitness from moral justification are not presented as UEOT inventions. The article's proposed contribution is their constrained integration through exact examples of failed transfer and a common-source compatibility witness. The internal source audit confirms what those examples say; it cannot establish that no prior author has proposed an equivalent philosophy.

The largest scholarly risks remain (1) whether the synthesis contributes enough beyond the closest organizational literature, (2) whether the additional realist commitment is preferable to an empiricist reading, (3) which natural systems instantiate the source relations, and (4) whether a stronger account can ground intrinsic biological functional norms. The manuscript explicitly leaves those questions open rather than assigning them a formal completion status.
