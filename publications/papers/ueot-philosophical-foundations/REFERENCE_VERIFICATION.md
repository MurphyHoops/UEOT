## Reuse rules

1. Cite a primary source for the claim it actually advances, and mark the UEOT author's disagreement or inference separately.
2. The first seven historical sources are background or direct conceptual predecessors, not evidence that UEOT is correct.
3. Bich, Corti, and Cuciniello show that the live debate includes regulation, normativity, and identity. An argument against an unqualified self-maintenance-to-purpose inference must acknowledge these strongest versions.
4. A relevant accessible passage was inspected for every substantive comparison. This does not mean all works were read cover to cover.
5. No third-party full text should be redistributed in the paper package. Bibliographic records, links, original analysis, and brief paraphrases suffice.
6. Article numbers in BibTeX are marked as such. The final citation style should not turn them into fictitious page ranges.

## Exclusions

No claim is made to have exhausted process philosophy, general systems theory, the history of cybernetics, every version of structural realism, or the current FEP literature. Whitehead, von Bertalanffy, and broader historical figures are not included merely to expand the bibliography. Additional sources should be added only for an actual argument not covered by the verified set. Neither search rankings nor citation counts were used as evidence of philosophical truth or priority.
