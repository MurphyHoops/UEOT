## Findings requiring editorial control

1. **Five kinds of meaning must not collapse in English.** Objecthood is not objectivity; a subject is not always an agent; functional significance is not experiential meaning; historical identity is not predictive equivalence; a controller’s value is not moral value. Appendix A and Chapters 5, 11, 13, 21, and 24 supply the distinctions but sometimes rely on the breadth of a Chinese word. Explicit English differentiation is required.

2. **Model-internal actuality is vulnerable to empirical inflation.** The repeated Chinese 真实/实际 establishes that outputs derive from the stated source, transactions, and histories rather than being added as assumptions. In English, “real-world” would instead suggest observations of external natural systems. Use “actual,” “realized,” “within the specified model,” or “derived from the same generative process” as appropriate. This applies especially to Chapters 7–12, 14, 17, 19, 28, and Appendix C.

3. **The growth factor and logarithmic growth rate must remain distinct.** The growth-classification note defines a least exponential base and a positive-root interpretation. Its critical value one is a growth factor of one, not an exponential rate of one. Equal asymptotic factors do not imply equal finite-horizon populations, expected payoffs, individual survival, or moral standing. Chapters 7, 11, 14, 15, 19, and 20 must preserve all these exclusions.

4. **The full policy comparison cannot be silently narrowed in translation.** “All permitted history-dependent real-randomized request policies” is stronger than a menu of deterministic programs. A translated simplification must keep permissions, temporal ordering before world noise, actual refusal of over-budget requests, and the common resource and inheritance rules. Any assumption of independent decisions by different members must come from the formal contract, not from English phrasing.

5. **Negative results need their quantifiers.** Full code erasure rules out reliable recovery of both different originals from the same present physical state; it does not establish that every recovery is impossible. Empty failure structure under a specified intervention family does not establish unconditional invulnerability. Indistinguishable short-horizon mechanisms do not mean that they are indistinguishable under every protocol. These are substantive philosophical boundaries, not optional technical qualifications.

6. **Normative claims should retain their open premises.** The positions on freedom, moral reasons, and shared life are philosophical defenses. English should not turn them into scientific findings merely by using “shows,” “demonstrates,” or “necessarily.” Conversely, weakening every defended claim to “perhaps” would misrepresent the Chinese argument. Each premise and the conclusion’s exact force should be preserved.

7. **Historical development must not overwrite current status.** Appendix C deliberately retains dated OPEN statements. Their English rendering must preserve their historical scope while clearly identifying the present contract. “Core” is sometimes a historical source text and sometimes the current mathematical program; references must identify which is intended.

8. **Specialized terms need explanations where they first carry argumentative weight.** In ordinary philosophical prose, “registered contract,” “consumer,” “source row,” “owner,” and “ledger” can read like unexplained software administration. Where the source means specified conditions, a theorem application, a response class, or a resource account, the English should say so. Technical identifiers in notes must remain exact and searchable.

9. **Authorship must remain transparent.** Degui Qian is the named original author and intellectual source. An editorial note should distinguish the current collaborative revision and AI-assisted translation from the original wording. This edition cannot imply that three AI reviews are human peer reviews, or that the English text has received external publication approval.

## Positive editorial assessment with limits

The baseline already sustains a coherent progression from persistence and relations to memory, action, evaluation, social life, and method. The strongest recurring distinction is between a condition of realization and an adequate reason for evaluation. The source also preserves meaningful negative results instead of interpreting every outcome as support for the theory. These strengths should survive stylistic revision.

The principal stylistic liability is the accumulation of consecutive model-status paragraphs in otherwise philosophical chapters. They can obscure the question the model is meant to answer. The remedy should be to state the philosophical contrast first, explain the minimal example in ordinary language, and retain exact implementation details in an explicitly signposted note or appendix. It would be inappropriate to omit results merely to make the translation shorter.
