# Early chapter scaffolds

`early-book-scaffolds.tar.gz` preserves the exact contents of the former
`book/README.md`, twelve short `book/chapters/` planning dossiers and
`source/paper/README.md`. These were outlines and routing notes, not a finished
book. Their original paths and hashes are listed in
[SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json](../SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json).

The old directories have been removed after this consolidation. The former
`source/paper/` PDF and ZIP entries were only symbolic links to the still-preserved
root files; their targets and hashes are recorded in the same manifest.

The five substantive extraction documents were moved byte for byte to
`philosophy/source/extracted/`. Active reading starts with the independently
maintained [philosophy](../../README.md) and
[mathematics](../../../mathematics/README.md) books.
