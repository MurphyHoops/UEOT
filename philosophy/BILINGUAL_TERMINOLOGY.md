# 中英术语与主张强度规范 / Bilingual Terminology and Claim Strength

Edition 3.0, 11 November 2026. Prepared by native AI editor C and adopted with revisions by the lead editor after full-text review. This is an internal editorial standard, not external peer review. The book’s scoped definitions govern every use.

## Governing choices

The English edition should read as a philosophical work written in English. Fidelity attaches to premises, conclusions, quantifiers, examples, and evidence status, not Chinese word order. No strengthening of a claim is permitted merely to make a sentence more fluent. A conceptual distinction that needs two English words takes precedence over a one-word bilingual mapping.

| Chinese expression | Preferred English | Editorial qualification |
|---|---|---|
| 客体（UEOT 的一般存在单位） | object | Lower case in running prose. The term does not by itself mean an inert material thing, an object of consciousness, or a passive counterpart to a subject. Explain its unusually broad scope at first substantive use. |
| 客体性／对象性 | objecthood | The conditions under which something counts as an object in the declared account. Avoid conflating an analytical criterion with a context-free metaphysical essence. |
| 对象（研究、意向或操作的所指） | object of inquiry / intentional object / target | Select by meaning; use “object” alone only where unambiguous. A controller’s target is not automatically an agent or a UEOT object. |
| 主体 | subject / agent | “Subject” for a bearer of experience or a standpoint; “agent” for an entity with action-guiding capacities. Neither translation establishes consciousness. |
| 主体性 | subjectivity / agency | Subjectivity concerns a perspective or experience; agency concerns capacities to act. Do not use interchangeably. |
| 关系身份 | relational identity | Use as the name of a defended account of how relations help constitute or sustain identity, not as an assertion that every relation is identity-constituting. |
| 身份／同一性 | identity | “Identity” for numerical or historical identity; “identity criterion” for a method of reidentification. Identity is not mere similarity. |
| 持续／续存 | persistence / continued existence | “Persistence” for philosophical continuity through change. “Survival” only where an explicit failure or death criterion is in play. |
| 组织 | organization | An arrangement of mutually relevant constraints and activities; not automatically an intentional institution. |
| 自我维持 | self-maintenance | A causal or functional claim whose boundary, resources, and relevant time scale must be explicit. Does not entail autonomy, reproduction, or moral worth. |
| 再生 | regeneration | Where components, capacities, or constraints are renewed. Translate reproduction separately. |
| 行动能力／能动性 | agency | A graded or specified capacity. “Action” and “agency” should not replace every instance of physical change. |
| 目的 | purpose / end / goal | “Purpose” for functional or explanatory directedness; “goal” for an operational target; “end” in philosophical discussion of ends. Do not infer intention from function. |
| 目的论 | teleology | Distinguish naturalized functional teleology, goal-directed control, and conscious intention. |
| 价值 | value | Mark the type: evaluative, instrumental, control-theoretic, biological, or moral. A value function is not a moral theory. |
| 评价 | evaluation / assessment | Evaluation can be operational without being morally authoritative. Use “valuation” only where a technical convention calls for it. |
| 规范性 | normativity | State whether the issue is functional success, epistemic warrant, practical reasons, or moral obligation. |
| 意义 | meaning / significance | Use “meaning in life” for the reflective and evaluative relation examined in Chapter 24, “meaning” for semantic or intentional content when that is the topic, and “significance” for importance. The capacity to reflect on meaning is not the threshold for moral standing. |
| 自由 | freedom | Distinguish available options, effective capacity, reflective self-government, political freedom, and metaphysical free will when the argument requires it. |
| 开放 | openness | Openness to material, energetic, informational, or social exchange as specified; not a free-standing proof of creativity or autonomy. |
| Ω | Ω; organization/integrity condition when explicitly defined | Preserve the symbol and the text’s declared use. Do not silently translate every Ω into a geometric loop or universal closure principle. |
| Π/Φ | Π/Φ; the proposed pair of drives | Preserve the symbols. State their current conditional or heuristic status; do not call them established universal physical laws. |
| GOD | Global Optimal Direction (GOD); an optimal-action correspondence where warranted | The expansion is attested in the original paper. Explain the historical name and then the currently authorized interpretation. Usually set-valued; not necessarily a gradient, unique direction, or command to pursue survival. The acronym has no theological force here. |
| GOA | Global Optimal Attractor (GOA); a family of long-run structures where warranted | The expansion is attested in the original paper. An attractor, invariant distribution, limit set, and conditional-survival structure are distinct mathematical objects. The historical name does not itself establish attraction. |
| 同源 | within the same generative process / derived from the same source | Never “homologous” unless biological homology is actually intended. The point is joint provenance and retained realization. |

## Claim-strength controls

“Can,” “may,” “under these conditions,” “in this model,” and “a possible account” must remain whenever they limit the Chinese claim. 必然 is not “typically”; 可能 is not “does”; 一种 is not “the”; 至少一种 is not “the unique.” A sufficient condition must not become necessary. A counterexample refutes the relevant universal claim but does not establish its unrestricted converse.

“Argument,” “account,” “interpretation,” and “proposal” should carry philosophical work. Reserve “proof,” “theorem,” and “formally verified” for identified mathematical results with their assumptions. A formally verified implication establishes its conclusion within its premises; it does not establish that the premises describe every natural object.

“Constitutive,” “causal,” “explanatory,” “predictive,” and “normative” denote different relations. The English edition must preserve those differences even where a smoother sentence would hide them. Functional contribution is not moral justification; evolutionary retention is not endorsement; reproduction is not the sole meaning of flourishing.

## Authorship and editorial disclosure

Use “Degui Qian (钱德桂)” as the author and originator named by the user. A transparent note may identify AI-assisted research, editing, translation, and cross-checking. Do not invent a translator’s personal name, human academic credentials, external peer review, publisher endorsement, or scholarly acceptance. “Editorial review by three native AI agents” accurately describes this process if the three reviews are actually completed.

## English prose

Prefer an explicit philosophical problem, a concrete discriminating case, a stated inference, and a serious objection over repeated programmatic announcements. Avoid habitual “not merely X but Y,” unearned “therefore,” inflated “universal” claims, and capitalized abstract nouns. Keep paragraph length responsive to the argument. Retain productive conceptual recurrence while removing repetition that performs no additional explanatory work.

## Title and part headings

Title of this edition: *Objects, Evolution, and Meaning: An Open Philosophy of Nature in Pursuit of Unity*. The subtitle treats unity as an inquiry rather than declaring all domains already unified.

1. Objects amid Change
2. Openness, Memory, and the Possibility of Action
3. Purpose, Evaluation, and Self-Revision
4. Composition, Scale, and Evolution
5. Mind, Freedom, and Shared Life
6. The Methods of Unification and Progress toward Truth

The sixth heading speaks of progress **toward** truth. Chapter 30 distinguishes historical progress in inquiry from the world-dependent truth of a claim; the English heading should not imply that truth itself changes as a result of agreement.
