# Total-source invariance under an internal transfer

This is an exact control calculation, separate from the original two-field static solution. Add two identical free real reservoir fields to two identical target fields, with the same minimal massless action and an internal O(4)-covariant renormalization prescription. A constant orthogonal rotation acts on every mode and its canonical momentum. The sum of the four stress tensors is unchanged as an operator-valued distribution. Consequently all smeared total-stress moments, responses and common-background influence functionals, whenever defined, are unchanged by rotating the state.

An internal swap can therefore lower target source variance while increasing reservoir variance by exactly the same amount, with no change to the complete gravitational matter input. This is stronger than a comparison of mean energies. It does not assert that a locally implemented rotation has no controller source.

For the uniform-angle product input let X=|P_T|^2/C, Y=|P_R|^2/C, Z=P_T.P_R/C. Write mu=L+2r, V=4rL+4r^2 and D=3/16. Then Var X=V+D, Var Y=V, E Z=0, Var Z=mu^2/2, and the mixed covariances vanish. Under P_T'=cos(theta)P_T+sin(theta)P_R and P_R'=-sin(theta)P_T+cos(theta)P_R, put z=sin(theta)^2. Direct expansion gives
\[
 V_T'=(1-z)^2(V+D)+z^2V+2\mu^2z(1-z),
\]
\[
 V_R'=z^2(V+D)+(1-z)^2V+2\mu^2z(1-z),
\]
\[
 C_{TR}'=z(1-z)(2V+D-2\mu^2).
\]
Their sum V_T'+V_R'+2C_TR' is exactly 2V+D. In particular, the large intermediate marginal variances are compensated by negative target/reservoir correlations. The swap endpoint transfers D without reducing the total source variance.

Adding reservoir fields changes the absolute mean source and the free-field curvature terms. The old two-field radius A is not automatically an on-shell four-field geometry. The operator identity is valid on a common prescribed background, or on any separately established common four-field solution. It is not a completed autonomous device or a new gravity law.
