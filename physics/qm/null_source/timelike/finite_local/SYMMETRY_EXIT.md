# What the new closed chain says about the original preparation goal

The present construction improves state/instrument/gravity consistency. It does not turn an internally directional preparation difference into an invariant gravity signal.

Assume the original two preparations have the same complete internal \(O(2)\) twirl, as in the inherited record-equivalence contract. Use the same two identical probe preparations, covariant dynamics, radial two-flag reader and an internally scalar controller. Then every invariant record probability is equal for the two preparations. More specifically, on the same prescribed geometry, each flag has the same total target stress mean, the same complete connected stress-noise kernel, and the same nonlocal retarded commutator input.

## Operator proof

Let \({\cal T}\) be the twirl and \({\cal I}_b\) the complete branch instrument. Covariance and an invariant flag imply
\[
{\cal I}_b{\cal T}={\cal T}{\cal I}_b.
\]
For every species-invariant observable \(A\), including well-defined smeared multi-time stress products and commutators,
\[
\operatorname{Tr}{\cal I}_b(\rho)A
 =\operatorname{Tr}{\cal I}_b({\cal T}\rho)A.
\]
Equal twirls therefore give equal unnormalized branch moments and equal branch probabilities. Division by the nonzero common probability gives equal conditional means and noises. The same argument applies to a complete sequence of covariant linear CP operations and invariant records, including all failure and stop events.

The finite kinetic interaction \(\sum_i\nabla\phi_i\cdot\nabla\psi_i\), identical adapted Gaussian probe modes, radial terminal reader, and scalar \(\chi\) action preserve this internal symmetry. Their spatial localization does not constitute an internal species reference. A real tensor geometry observable is also an internal singlet.

## The actual kernels make the equality concrete

In CONDITIONAL_TENSOR.md and CONDITIONAL_NOISE.md, \(P_b\), \(E_bX\) and \(\operatorname{Cov}_bX\) depend only on the shared radial law \(R\) and the common noise \(\delta\). The mode functions \(q,c,u\) are the same for both species. Thus the equality is explicit in the computed mean, noise and Kubo inputs, rather than a hope that finer instruments will eventually resolve a tiny difference.

A causal response built from these same invariant inputs and one common physical rule cannot attribute a difference to the original angular preparation alone. The present protocol's field energy injection and nonzero anisotropic spatial stress are apparatus consequences shared by the equivalent preparations.

## Scope of the conclusion

The theorem requires the complete common twirl and the linear CP operational contract. Covariance alone does not constrain arbitrary nonlinear state selectors: the script shows a pure state and its rotational mixture have the same twirl but different purity. A rule that feeds such an unobserved nonlinear state functional into geometry would require a new ensemble-consistent physical law; it is not supplied by saying that the rule is covariant.

A different invariant source law, an explicit species reference, or an internally symmetry-breaking apparatus changes the experimental contract. Its reference state, energy, stress, records and calibration must be counted. Neither adding a scalar controller nor increasing the precision of the current radial reader makes those resources appear.

This is a closed negative result for the stated preparation-to-gravity route. It is not a no-go theorem for unification in general. The next research objective must be a consistent total-source model and a genuinely distinct invariant prediction, rather than continued attempts to distinguish preparations whose complete allowed records are already equal.
