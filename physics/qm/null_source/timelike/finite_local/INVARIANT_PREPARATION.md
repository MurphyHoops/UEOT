## An autonomous preparation must close the total-energy ledger

There is a precise next test beyond specifying a normal low-noise target state. On a fixed stationary background, suppose a closed matter-plus-apparatus preparation has the same initial complete spectral law of its total Hamiltonian in both protocols, and each complete evolution commutes with that Hamiltonian. For every bounded spectral function f,
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}\rho_{A,\rm out} f(H_{\rm tot})
 =\operatorname{Tr}\rho_{B,\rm out} f(H_{\rm tot})
 =\operatorname{Tr}\rho_{\rm in} f(H_{\rm tot}).
\]
This follows by spectral conservation, without a Gaussian approximation. Different program states are permitted only if their actual initial total-energy laws meet the stated condition.

If the interaction vanishes at the endpoint, H_tot=H_target+H_device. With E_s=C/(2A^3), the target variance reduction from the original two-ring law to the matched quiet law is (3/16)E_s^2. Under this additional autonomous contract it must be balanced by
\[
 \Delta\operatorname{Var}H_{\rm device}
 +2\Delta\operatorname{Cov}(H_{\rm target},H_{\rm device})
 =+(3/16)E_s^2
\]
when Delta means quiet minus original. Target preparation alone therefore does not prove reduced complete apparatus energy noise.

This is a stationary-background matter-energy ledger, not the general-relativistic Hamiltonian constraint and not a universal no-go for gravity experiments. Local stress distributions may change; different actual controller resources, work exchange or backgrounds require their own ledger. The present new preparations have not been derived as autonomous transformations of the old actual input plus the same closed apparatus state.
