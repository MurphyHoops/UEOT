## 1. Resolving Sharp-Slice Domain Restrictions via Spacetime-Smeared ADM Projections

`NEXT_GATE.md` Gate 2 notes that at $K = \infty$ and finite $g > 0$, sharp-slice $\mathcal{Q}(H_{0,3}(X))$ forms must be superseded by spacetime-smeared ADM constraint projections (`RC10` proved sharp-slice minimal-clock pressure variance blows up as $\frac{2}{45}K^5$). $$\mathrm{Var}_{\Psi_{\mathrm{actual},\infty}}\!\bigl(\hat{\mathcal{C}}_{\mathrm{ADM}}[N, 0]\bigr) = (8\pi G)^2\,\mathrm{Var}_{\Psi_{\mathrm{actual},\infty}}\!\left(\int d^4x\sqrt{-\bar g}\,N\,\hat T_{0,\mathrm{ren}}^{0,\mathrm{tot},\rho_0,\infty}\right).$$
Using `RC10`'s uniform $K \le \infty$ spacetime-smeared total energy variance $\sigma_{E,\mathrm{smear},\infty}^2 \in [1.23359\times 10^{80}, 1.23371\times 10^{80}]$ on $\tau \in [1/2, 5/2]$ and `RC05`'s $8\pi G = \frac{3}{5}g^2 = 6\times 10^{-81}$ ($g = 10^{-40}$), the dimensionless gravitational ADM constraint variance and RMS fluctuation are **uniformly bounded for all $K \le \infty$ without sharp-slice domain restrictions**:
