# AH07 — Localized Autonomous Clock Initial Data in Asymptotically Flat ($\Lambda = 0$) Spacetime

## 1. Eliminating Cosmological Closure Density (`NEXT_GATE` Gate 3)

On compact $S^3$ (`RC05`, `RC12`), a spatially homogeneous clock mode fills the entire closed 3-sphere and forces $\Lambda > 0$ plus closure-scale density $\varepsilon_X \sim \frac{c^4}{G R_{S^3}^2}$ to avoid a $\Delta\tau < \pi$ Big Crunch. Here we prove that replacing $(S^3, \Lambda > 0)$ by **asymptotically flat $\mathbb{R}^{1,3}$ with $\Lambda = 0$** and a compactly supported clock/apparatus wavepacket inside $B_{R_{\mathrm{app}}} \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ admits a unique, smooth, horizon-free nonlinear ADM initial-data solution whenever bare compactness satisfies $\mathcal{C}_0 < \frac{2048}{9375}$.

## 2. Exact Rational Sub-/Super-Solution and Banach Contraction Theorem for $-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^3}\psi = \frac{2\pi G}{c^2}\rho(x)\psi^5$

On a time-symmetric slice ($K_{ij} = 0$, $\Lambda = 0$, $g_{ij} = \psi(x)^4\delta_{ij}$, $\psi(x) \to 1$ as $|x| \to \infty$), the full nonlinear Einstein Hamiltonian constraint reduces to Lichnerowicz's elliptic equation $-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^3}\psi = \frac{2\pi G}{c^2}\rho(x)\psi^5$, where $\rho(x) := \varepsilon(x)/c^2 \in C^{0,\beta}_c(B_{R_{\mathrm{app}}})$ is rest-mass-equivalent energy density ($\varepsilon(x)$ in $\text{J}/\text{m}^3$). Suppose $0 \le \rho(x) \le \rho_0 \mathbf{1}_{|x| \le R_{\mathrm{app}}}$ ($\rho \not\equiv 0$), let $M_{\mathrm{coord}} := \int_{\mathbb{R}^3}\rho(x)\,d^3x \le M_0 := \frac{4}{3}\pi R_{\mathrm{app}}^3 \rho_0$, and define $\mathcal{C}_0 := \frac{2 G M_0}{c^2 R_{\mathrm{app}}} = 4\alpha$. Set $\psi_{\max} := \frac{5}{4}$ and define the radial comparison barrier
$$\psi_+(r) := \begin{cases} 1 + \alpha\psi_{\max}^5\bigl(\frac{3}{2} - \frac{r^2}{2 R_{\mathrm{app}}^2}\bigr), & 0 \le r \le R_{\mathrm{app}}, \\ 1 + \alpha\psi_{\max}^5\frac{R_{\mathrm{app}}}{r}, & r > R_{\mathrm{app}}. \end{cases}$$

1. **Exact $C^1(\mathbb{R}^3)$ regularity and distributional supersolution**: At $r = R_{\mathrm{app}}$, both branches give $\psi_+(R_{\mathrm{app}}) = 1 + \alpha\psi_{\max}^5$ and $\psi_+'(R_{\mathrm{app}}) = -\alpha\psi_{\max}^5/R_{\mathrm{app}}$. On $r < R_{\mathrm{app}}$, $-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^3}\psi_+ = \frac{3\alpha\psi_{\max}^5}{R_{\mathrm{app}}^2} = \frac{2\pi G}{c^2}\rho_0\psi_{\max}^5$, while on $r > R_{\mathrm{app}}$, $-\Delta_{\mathbb{R}^3}\psi_+ = 0$.
2. **Sharp rational threshold $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{crit}} = \frac{2048}{9375}$ and strict Banach contraction**: Maximizing $\frac{2(\psi_{\max}-1)}{3\psi_{\max}^5}$ yields $\psi_{\max} = \frac{5}{4}$ and $\alpha_{\mathrm{crit}} = \frac{512}{9375}$ ($\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{crit}} = \frac{2048}{9375} \approx 0.218453$). Whenever $0 < \mathcal{C}_0 < \frac{2048}{9375}$, $\psi_+(0) < \frac{5}{4}$, and the Newtonian integral operator $\mathcal{T}(\psi)(x) := 1 + \frac{G}{2c^2}\int_{\mathbb{R}^3}\frac{\rho(y)\psi(y)^5}{|x-y|}\,d^3y$ maps $\mathcal{D} := \{\psi \in C_b(\mathbb{R}^3) : 1 \le \psi(x) \le \psi_+(|x|)\}$ into itself with strict $L^\infty$ Lipschitz constant
   $$L_{\mathcal{T}} \le \left(\frac{3}{2}\alpha\right)\left(5\psi_{\max}^4\right) = \frac{15}{2}\alpha\left(\frac{5}{4}\right)^4 = \frac{9375}{2048}\mathcal{C}_0 = \frac{\mathcal{C}_0}{\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{crit}}} < 1.$$
   By Banach contraction and interior/exterior Schauder regularity, $\mathcal{T}$ has a unique fixed point $\psi \in C^{2,\beta}(\mathbb{R}^3)\cap\mathcal{D}$ satisfying coordinate and proper ADM mass brackets $M_{\mathrm{coord}} < M_{\mathrm{ADM}} = \int_{\mathbb{R}^3}\rho(x)\psi(x)^5\,d^3x \le \frac{3125}{1024} M_{\mathrm{coord}}$ and $\frac{4}{5}M_{\mathrm{proper}} < M_{\mathrm{ADM}} = M_{\mathrm{proper}} - \frac{c^2}{2\pi G}\int_{\mathbb{R}^3}|\nabla\psi|^2\,d^3x < M_{\mathrm{proper}}$ ($M_{\mathrm{proper}} := \int_{\mathbb{R}^3}\rho\psi^6\,d^3x$).
3. **Strict absence of minimal / trapped surfaces**: Since $\alpha\psi_{\max}^5 < \frac{512}{9375}\cdot\frac{3125}{1024} = \frac{1}{6}$, any radial solution $\psi \in \mathcal{D}$ satisfies $0 \le -r\psi'(r) = \frac{2\pi G}{c^2 r}\int_0^r s^2\rho(s)\psi(s)^5\,ds \le -r\psi_+'(r) < \frac{1}{6}$, so the areal-radius derivative obeys $\frac{d}{dr}\bigl(r\psi(r)^2\bigr) = \psi(r)\bigl(\psi(r) + 2r\psi'(r)\bigr) > 1\cdot(1 - \frac{1}{3}) = \frac{2}{3} > 0$ for all $r \ge 0$ (and $\frac{d}{dr}(r\psi_+^2) \ge \frac{35}{36} > 0$).

## 3. Localized Massive Clock Dispersion Control

Choosing clock carrier mass $M_X > 0$ and localized wavepacket radius $R_{\mathrm{app}} \ge 10 L_{\mathrm{sensor}}$ bounds free wavepacket spreading over operating duration $T_{\mathrm{exp}}$ by $\Delta R_{\mathrm{disp}} \le \frac{\hbar T_{\mathrm{exp}}}{M_X R_{\mathrm{app}}}$, keeping the localized clock strictly sub-horizon ($\mathcal{C}_0 \ll \frac{2048}{9375}$) in flat space with $\Lambda = 0$.
