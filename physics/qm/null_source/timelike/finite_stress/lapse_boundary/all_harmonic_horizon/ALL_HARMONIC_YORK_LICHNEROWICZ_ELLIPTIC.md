## 1. Universal $(\ell - 1)(\ell + 3)$ Invertibility Across All Three York–Lichnerowicz Sectors

With $\ell = 0$ solved by the sub-critical bounce $a(\tau)$ (`RC06`) and all 14 $\ell = 1$ conformal + $SO(4)$ Killing obstructions matched on $\Sigma_{\pi^+}$ (`RC08`, `AH03`, `AH04`), consider the remaining infinite-dimensional $\ell \ge 2$ ($k = \ell + 1 \ge 3$) continuum limiting/parametrix total stress-energy components on $\tau = \pi^+$.

Adopt **constant-mean-curvature (CMC) slice gauge** $\delta K_\ell = 0$ ($\ell \ge 2$) with $g_{ij} = a(\pi)^2(1 + 2\psi)\gamma_{ij}$ and shift covector $N_i = a(\pi)^3(\nabla_i B + W_i)$ ($\nabla^i W_i = 0$ wrt $\gamma_{ij}$). Decompose the physical ADM momentum vector $\delta j^i := \bar g^{im}(-n^\mu\delta T_{\mu m}) = \gamma^{im}\nabla_m\delta q_{\mathrm{tot}} + \delta j_{\perp,\mathrm{tot}}^i$ ($\nabla_i\delta j_{\perp,\mathrm{tot}}^i = 0$). Because isotropic FRW has $\bar A_{ij} \equiv 0$, $\delta(\tilde A_{ij}\tilde A^{ij} - \frac{2}{3}K^2) = -\frac{4}{3}\bar K\delta K = 0$ even at $H_p(\pi) \neq 0$. Linearizing $R^{(3)}(a^2(1+2\psi)\gamma) = a^{-2}[6 - 12\psi - 4\Delta_{S^3}\psi]$ and $\bar D_j\delta\tilde A_i{}^j = \frac{1}{2}\nabla^j(\mathcal{L}_{N^{\mathrm{unit}}}\gamma)_{ij}$ on unit $S^3$ ($R_{ij}(\gamma) = 2\gamma_{ij}$, $-\Delta_{S^3} Y_\ell = \ell(\ell+2)Y_\ell$, rough Laplacian $-\Delta_{S^3} W_\ell = [\ell(\ell+2)-1]W_\ell \implies \frac{1}{2}\nabla^j(\nabla_i W_{\ell,j}+\nabla_j W_{\ell,i}) = -\frac{1}{2}(\ell-1)(\ell+3)W_{\ell,i}$) yields **closed-form algebraic inversion for every $\ell \ge 2$**:
$$\psi_\ell = \frac{4\pi G a(\pi)^2}{(\ell - 1)(\ell + 3)}\,\delta\rho_{\mathrm{tot},\ell}, \qquad B_\ell = -\frac{12\pi G a(\pi)^2}{(\ell - 1)(\ell + 3)}\,\delta q_{\mathrm{tot},\ell}, \qquad W_\ell^i = -\frac{16\pi G a(\pi)^2}{(\ell - 1)(\ell + 3)}\,\delta j_{\perp,\mathrm{tot},\ell}^i,$$
where $(\ell - 1)(\ell + 3) \ge 5$ for all $\ell \ge 2$.

## 2. Exact Closed-Form Scalar and Vector 3-Sphere Sobolev–Green Constants

By the Unsöld addition theorem on unit $S^3$ ($V_0 = 2\pi^2$), $\|\psi_\ell\|_{L^\infty(S^3)} \le \frac{\ell + 1}{\sqrt{2}\pi}\|\psi_\ell\|_{L^2(S^3)}$, while for divergence-free vector harmonics (multiplicity $2\ell(\ell+2) < 2(\ell+1)^2$) $|W_\ell(x)|_\gamma \le \frac{\sqrt{2}(\ell+1)}{\sqrt{2}\pi}\|W_\ell\|_{L^2(S^3)}$. Cauchy–Schwarz across $\ell \ge 2$ gives:
$$\|\psi_{\ge 2}\|_{L^\infty(S^3)} \le 4\pi G a(\pi)^2 C_{\mathrm{Sob},S^3}\|\delta\rho_{\mathrm{tot},\ge 2}\|_{L^2(S^3)}, \qquad C_{\mathrm{Sob},S^3} := \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\pi}\sqrt{\sum_{\ell=2}^\infty \frac{(\ell + 1)^2}{(\ell - 1)^2(\ell + 3)^2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\pi}\sqrt{\frac{\pi^2}{12} - \frac{55}{576}},$$
and $C_{\mathrm{Sob,vec},S^3} := \sqrt{2}\,C_{\mathrm{Sob},S^3}$. By orthogonal Hodge decomposition on $S^3$ ($\ell(\ell+2) \ge 8$ for $\ell \ge 2$), $\|\delta q_{\mathrm{tot},\ge 2}\|_{H^2} \le \frac{1}{\sqrt{8}}\|\delta j_{\mathrm{tot},\ge 2}\|_{H^2}$ and $\|\delta j_{\perp,\mathrm{tot},\ge 2}\|_{H^2} \le \|\delta j_{\mathrm{tot},\ge 2}\|_{H^2}$. Since $\max_{k \ge 3}\frac{k^2}{k^2 - 4} = \frac{9}{5}$ ($k = \ell + 1$), elliptic inversion gains $+2$ derivatives with exact factor $\frac{9}{5}$: $\|(1-\Delta_{S^3})^2 u_{\ge 2}\|_{L^2} \le \frac{9}{5}\|(1-\Delta_{S^3})[(\ell-1)(\ell+3)u_{\ge 2}]\|_{L^2}$, so $\delta\rho_{\mathrm{tot},\ge 2}, \delta j_{\mathrm{tot},\ge 2} \in H^2(S^3)$ implies $(\psi_{\ge 2}, B_{\ge 2}, W_{\ge 2}^i) \in H^4(S^3) \hookrightarrow C^{2,1/2}(S^3) \subset C^2(S^3)$.
