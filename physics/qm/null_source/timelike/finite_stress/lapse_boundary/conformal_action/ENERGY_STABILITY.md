# E. The nondegenerate relational generator has no lower bound

This is a statement about the selected u-clock generator in
ANOMALY_ACTION, not a complete gauge-invariant four-dimensional ghost
classification. It is nevertheless a necessary limitation on claims of
a stable ground state in this construction.

Let n_s>0, g=lambda-n_s/240 !=0, alpha=1/(2g), and
d3=n_s/720-lambda/2. Fix one source-energy eigenvector.

If g<0, take real normalized f in C_c infinity((a,b)), a>0, and
psi_n(kappa)=exp(i n kappa) f(kappa).

    <H>_n=alpha n² integral |f|²/kappa + C.

The linear term vanishes because f is real. Hence <H>_n->-infinity.

    <d3 kappa³>=d3 R³+O(R²).

operator domain; they do not rely on an unproved operator core.
Transporting the scalar endpoint phase transports these witnesses
and cannot change the spectral conclusion.

a ground energy for its u generator. Unitary dynamics, a positive
physical inner product, and normal records in JOINT_CONTINUUM remain
valid; they are different properties.
