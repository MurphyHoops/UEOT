# RC10 — Sharp-Slice Minimal-Clock Pressure Variance Divergence ($O(K^5)$) versus Uniform Spacetime-Smeared Continuum Total Stress Variance

## Scope and Results

In four-dimensional continuum quantum field theory, general stress-energy components are operator-valued distributions on spacetime rather than sharp-time operators with finite variance (even though special conformally improved charges such as the conformal-Killing dipole $A_Z^{\Phi_+}$ of `SHARP_CHARGES` achieve $O(1)$ sharp-slice cancellation). Here we prove both sides of this boundary for the renormalized continuum apparatus + body + Parseval frame + pointer system (`RC06`–`RC09`):

1. **Exact $O(K^5)$ Sharp-Slice Divergence of Minimal-Clock Spatial Pressure**: For the minimally coupled massless clock $X$ ($\xi = 0, M_X = 0$) on unit $S^3$, the integrated spatial pressure trace on a constant-time slice $\tau_0$ is $\int_{S^3} :\!\gamma^{ij} T_{ij,X}^{(K)}(\tau_0, x)\!:\,dV_\gamma = \sum_{k=2}^K\sum_{m=1}^{k^2} :\!\big(\frac{3}{2} p_{X,k,m}^2 - \frac{1}{2}\omega_{X,k}^2 q_{X,k,m}^2\big)\!:$ ($\omega_{X,k} = \sqrt{k^2 - 1}$). Unlike the conformally improved dipole $A_Z^{\Phi_+}$ (`SHARP_CHARGES` Eq. (3)), here the kinetic $-\frac{3}{4}\omega_{X,k}$ and gradient $-\frac{1}{4}\omega_{X,k}$ pair-creation coefficients have the **same sign** and add to $-\omega_{X,k}(a_{X,k,m}^\dagger)^2$, yielding the **exact** vacuum variance identity
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_{\Omega_X}\!\left(\int_{S^3} :\!p_X^{(K)}(\tau_0, x)\!:\,dV_\gamma\right) = \frac{1}{9}\operatorname{Var}_{\Omega_X}\!\left(\int_{S^3} :\!\gamma^{ij} T_{ij,X}^{(K)}(\tau_0, x)\!:\,dV_\gamma\right) = \frac{2}{9}\sum_{k=2}^K k^2(k^2 - 1) \asymp \frac{2}{45}\,K^5 \xrightarrow{K \to \infty} +\infty! \tag{1}
\]

2. **Uniform Spacetime-Smeared Continuum Stress Variance ($\sup_{K \le \infty}\operatorname{Var}(T_{\mu\nu}^{(K)}(f)) < \infty$)**: Let $\chi_t \in C_c^\infty([1/2, 5/2])$ be the unit-integral temporal convolution bump obtained by dilating `QUANTIFIED_PREPARATION`'s $b$ from $[-1/5, 1/5]$ (width $2/5$) by factor $5$ to width $2$, so $\|\chi_t^{(4)}\|_{L^1} = 5^{-4} B_4 < \frac{1517824}{625} < 2429$. Because every oscillatory mode across all $11$ scalar species ($2$ body + $6$ conformal Parseval frame + $1$ clock + $1$ pointer + $1$ beacon) satisfies $\omega_{k_1} + \omega_{k_2} \ge \sqrt{3}\,\min(k_1, k_2)$ (accounting for $\omega_{-,1} = \sqrt{4/5}$ and $\omega_{X,k} = \sqrt{k^2 - 1} \ge \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} k$ on $k \ge 2$), temporal integration suppresses pair creation by $|\widehat\chi_t(\omega_{k_1} + \omega_{k_2})|^2 \le B_{t,4}^2 (\sqrt{3}\,k)^{-8} = \frac{B_{t,4}^2}{81\,k^8}$:
\[
 \sum_{k=1}^\infty k^4\,\frac{B_{t,4}^2}{81\,k^8} = \frac{B_{t,4}^2}{81}\,\zeta(4) = \frac{\pi^4 B_{t,4}^2}{7290} < \infty. \tag{2}
\]
Testing against the explicit covariant energy test tensor $f^{\mu\nu}(\tau, x) = V_0^{-1} a(\tau)^{-2}\chi_t(\tau)\,\delta^\mu_\tau\delta^\nu_\tau$ (so $\int d^4x\sqrt{-\bar g}\,f^{\mu\nu} T_{\mu\nu}^X = \int\frac{\chi_t(\tau)}{a(\tau)^2}\,d\tau\cdot\frac{1}{2 V_0} p_{X,0}^2$ with canonical zero-mode mean $\bar p_{X,0} = \frac{\sqrt{V_0}}{g}$ and variance $\frac{1}{4 a_0}$, or integrated energy functional $V_0 f^{\mu\nu}$ giving $\ge \frac{V_0}{4 a_0 g^2 a_{\max}^4}$) bounds $\sup_{K \le \infty}\operatorname{Var}_{\Psi_K}\big(\int d^4x\sqrt{-\bar g}\,V_0 f^{\mu\nu}\hat T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{tot},(K)}\big)$ inside a finite $K$-independent outward interval!
