This closes more than an assigned covariance example. It still leaves

Core remains unchanged. No numerical pass count, positive normal Gram
kernel or local transfer theorem is a derivation of all physical laws,
an empirical calibration, or a completed physical unification.

## 2. A concrete obstruction to the next naive geometric repair

This section is a **frozen-source, linearized geometric diagnostic**.
It does not assume AP/AQ is already a self-consistent Einstein background.
Subtract a hypothetically satisfied zeroth-order Hamiltonian constraint
on the unit round \(S^3\), with \(K_0=0\), fixed \(\Lambda\), and freeze
the density response except for the prescribed AR increment.

Use \(\Delta=\nabla^i\nabla_i\) (negative eigenvalues). For an arbitrary
smooth symmetric metric variation \(h\), not only a conformal one,
\[
 Lh:=DR_\gamma[h]=\nabla^i\nabla^j h_{ij}
          -\Delta\operatorname{tr}h-2\operatorname{tr}h,
\quad
 L^*Y=\nabla_i\nabla_jY-\gamma_{ij}\Delta Y-2Y\gamma_{ij}.
\]
For each of the four first scalar harmonics \(Y_A\),
\(\nabla_i\nabla_jY_A=-Y_A\gamma_{ij}\) and \(\Delta Y_A=-3Y_A\).
Therefore \(L^*Y_A=0\), and every smooth solution of \(Lh=s\) must obey
\[
 \int_{S^3}Y_A s\,dV=0\quad(A=1,\ldots,4).
\]
It is also sufficient for this frozen smooth linear problem: if all
four moments vanish, set \(h=2u\gamma\) with
\[
 u_{\ell a}={s_{\ell a}\over4[\ell(\ell+2)-3]}
 \quad(\ell\ne1),\qquad u_{1a}=0.
\]
Elliptic spectral decay makes \(u\) smooth and \(Lh=s\).
For example an antipodally balanced density passes this frozen test;
this is not a full quantum-matter/gravity construction.

This is a range condition for every metric variation. The conformal
special case \(h=2u\gamma\) makes it visible as
\(Lh=-4(\Delta+3)u\). Projecting away the first harmonics changes the
specified source; it does not solve its constraint.

Scale the coherent source momentum by \(\eta f\), so that its density
increment is \(\eta^2 f^2/2\). Try only
\(\gamma_\eta=\gamma+\eta^2h+o(\eta^2)\) and
\(K_\eta=O(\eta^2)\), with no additional matter response.
The Hamiltonian constraint
\(R+(\operatorname{tr}K)^2-|K|^2=16\pi G_N\rho+2\Lambda\)
would require, at order \(\eta^2\),
\[
 Lh=8\pi G_N f^2 .
\]
Let \(Y_c(y)=\cos d(c,y)\), where \(c\) is the AR source-cap center.
Then
\[
 \int Y_c\,{f^2\over2}\,dV
 \ge {1\over2}\cos(1/2)e^{-2/3}
                {V_{1/4}\over V_{1/2}}>0 .
\]
The left side of the metric equation has zero \(Y_c\) projection,
while its right side is strictly positive for \(G_N>0\).
**This particular frozen-density linear repair is rejected.**
A uniform radius shift, pure coordinate change or uniform cosmological
term cannot supply this missing first-harmonic component.

The order convention matters: if the small parameter is the density
\(\epsilon=\eta^2\), the rejected ansatz has metric and extrinsic
curvature \(O(\epsilon)\). An extrinsic curvature \(O(\eta)\), a metric
kernel component whose quadratic term contributes, a different
correlated preparation, or the actual metric dependence of the quantum
density changes the problem. None is excluded here, and none has been
constructed here. All must also satisfy the momentum constraints.

This diagnostic uses the classical scalar-curvature derivative and
AR's exact code-minus-reference density. It is **not** a theorem that
the full semiclassical or quantum Einstein constraint has this same
adjoint kernel. Its source, initial density operator, normal vector,
volume element, allowed counterterms and apparatus all vary in the
actual problem.

Primary source for \(DR\), its adjoint and the sphere's first-harmonic
kernel: Fischer–Marsden, *Deformations of the scalar curvature*,
Duke Math. J. 42 (1975) 519–547, Section 2, Lemma 2 and pages 526–527,
https://www.cds.caltech.edu/~marsden/bib/1975/02-FiMa1975/FiMa1975.pdf .
Their positive Laplacian is opposite to the convention above.

## 3. The next minimum deliverable

Before interpreting the frozen test as a physical constraint, choose a
zeroth-order background that actually satisfies the required constraints.
For a stated same-state continuation, write schematically
\[
 \delta\rho=\rho_{\rm enc}+\mathcal R_{\rm matter}[h,k]
                 +\delta\rho_{\rm initial}+\delta\rho_{\rm apparatus}.
\]
The true scalar block then involves \(L-16\pi G_N\mathcal R_{\rm matter}\),
together with the other response columns and momentum equations.
Its adjoint kernel must be computed; the frozen \(L^*\) kernel cannot
simply be carried over. If \(O(\eta)\) metric/extrinsic-curvature data
are allowed, retain
\(\tfrac12D^2R[h_1,h_1]+(\operatorname{tr}K_1)^2-|K_1|^2\)
and the corresponding second-order matter response at order \(\eta^2\).

The next item is the **same-state joint lapse/constraint response of
the local prepared system with its actual source and readout stress**.
The task is more precise than adding an independent geometric random
variable, changing a density by hand, or inverting only the homogeneous
constraint.

Retain a common finite-slice density operator and both CTP lapses,
the spatial metric and shift until after variation. The first deliverable
must contain these items together:

1. A stated total local action for geometry, source, probe, preparation
   actuator and readout. If the AL nonlinear local-field candidate is
   used, supply its own normal correlated preparation and continuum
   domain; the exact AQ linear state is not its solution. If an
   external-source comparison is retained, label the missing apparatus
   response explicitly and do not sign a closed Einstein source.
2. A single renormalization datum that includes finite gravitational,
   mixed-source and boundary terms, not only the scalar ESU \(e(r)\).
   Fix what is held constant during lapse/metric/source variation.
3. The exact common-cutoff constraint columns and initial mixed blocks.
   Include the variation of the prepared density operator, the normal
   and spatial volume, spectral projectors if used, interaction stress,
   and the source/readout impulses. AQ's \(C_{Nr},C_{rr}\) are actual
   homogeneous cutoff checks, not the whole tensor response.
4. The smooth spacetime-domain full-field limit of that response and
   its noise on the same positive normal joint state. A real local
   contact can cancel a response divergence without subtracting noise;
   a sharp variance cannot be certified by a different smeared object.
5. Solve both Hamiltonian and momentum constraints and the causal
   pressure/evolution equations on a strictly positive window.
   Test at least the four first spatial harmonics as well as the
   homogeneous charge. Any incoming geometric or preparation term
   must arise from that action/state, with its actual covariance.
6. Couple a bounded, local readout effect to that solved joint history.
   Show which physical observable it calibrates, include the device's
   energy/stress and causal collection time, and retain every outcome.
   The AR sign contrast is not automatically a constraint-charge

The mean and fluctuation levels remain separate gates. A common mean

record does not supply a completed autonomous classical apparatus.

## 4. Stop conditions and useful failure outputs

Immediately reject a proposal that keeps the ruled-out thin-source
full bare marginal while assigning an independent carrier its missing
charge. Do not reopen that candidate without changing its contract.

Reject a cutoff proof that silently moves physical bare-tail vectors
into the fixed full-energy graph. Use actual varying-operator recovery,
or clearly introduce a different common-diagonal auxiliary sequence.

Reject a geometric repair that drops a nonzero first-harmonic forcing
projection. If the full coupled operator changes the obstruction,
derive that change from the actual state/metric response.

If the local nonlinear continuum state or autonomous device remains
unconstructed, report that exact gap rather than promote formal
one-loop closure, fixed-cutoff positivity or a global memory to a
completed device. Preserve the working linear comparisons as tests for
the eventual construction.
