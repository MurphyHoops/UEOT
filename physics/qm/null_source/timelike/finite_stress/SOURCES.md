# Primary sources and their role

- V. Husain and T. Pawlowski, “Dust reference frame in quantum cosmology,”
  arXiv:1108.1147. https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1147
  Section 3.2 supplies a square-root-volume difference-ordering precedent.
  The current sign follows our declared positive-dust constraint.
- P. Singh and E. Wilson-Ewing, “Quantization Ambiguities and Bounds on
  Geometric Scalars in Anisotropic Loop Quantum Cosmology,”
  arXiv:1310.6728v2. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.6728
  Equations (2.44) and (4.9) distinguish K quantization and a spectral inverse.
- A. Ashtekar, T. Pawlowski, P. Singh and K. Vandersloot,
  “Loop quantum cosmology of k=1 FRW models,” gr-qc/0612104.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0612104
  This F-quantization model is not silently identified with our candidate.
- G. Teschl, “Jacobi Operators and Completely Integrable Nonlinear Lattices,”
  AMS Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 72.
  https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-jac/jacop.pdf
  Sections 2.5–2.6 provide spectral/Jacobi and unbounded-domain background.
- M. Radzikowski, “Micro-local approach to the Hadamard condition in quantum
  field theory on curved space-time,” CMP 179 (1996).
  https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02100096
  The finite-mode smooth-difference statement is used only on fixed classical backgrounds.
- C. J. Fewster et al., “Quantum Reference Frames, Measurement Schemes and
  the Type of Local Algebras in Quantum Field Theory,” CMP 406, 19 (2025).
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00220-024-05180-7
  Section 3.1, Example 3 gives the semibounded-clock time-POVM framework.
- B. L. Hu, A. Roura and E. Verdaguer, “Induced quantum metric fluctuations
  and the validity of semiclassical gravity,” gr-qc/0402029.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0402029
  Its intrinsic/induced and initial-state discussion informs the next gate;
  a leading large-N result is not imported as an exact two-field theorem.

The new conjunctions, exclusions, constructions, intervals and scope
decisions in this directory are project research arguments. These sources
do not certify UEOT physical unification or empirical success.

- W. Kaminski, “The volume operator in loop quantum cosmology,” arXiv:1906.07554.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.07554
  This is prior evidence for volume-domain problems in different flat scalar-clock models,
  not a theorem for our closed positive-dust family. No first-discovery claim is made.

- P. R. Anderson, C. Molina-Paris and E. Mottola, "Linear Response, Validity of Semi-Classical Gravity, and the Stability of Flat Space," gr-qc/0209075.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0209075
  Equations (3.4)-(3.9) and the page-8 discussion require the retarded commutator and local contact terms together.
- S. Hollands and R. M. Wald, "Conservation of the stress tensor in perturbative interacting quantum field theory in curved spacetimes," gr-qc/0404074.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0404074
  Section 4.3 and Theorem 5.1 connect covariant metric variation and the Ward identity. They do not establish our uncomputed boundary extension.

## Finite-slice and updated tensor literature checked in this continuation

- Juarez-Aubry, Kay, Miramontes and Sudarsky, arXiv:2205.11671: the general nonlinear surface-Hadamard initial-value proposal is explicitly a conjecture; its constraints are part of the required data. https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11671
- Meda, Pinamonti and Siemssen, arXiv:2007.14665: a flat massive FLRW local mean-SCE theorem with compatible data, not the present closed massless full-CTP contract. https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.14665
- Juarez-Aubry and Modak, arXiv:2110.01719v2, Theorem 7: a coupled conformally-static result with constrained fourth-order metric data and a massless conformally coupled field. The minimal coupling in our model is different. https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01719
- Galanda, Meda, Murro, Pinamonti and Schmid, arXiv:2604.01047v1, submitted 1 April 2026: a tensor linearized Minkowski-vacuum forcing theorem for past-compact metric/state perturbations. It supplies a relevant new nonlocal response result, not a closed-S3 nonlinear joint CTP solution. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01047
- Cortez et al., arXiv:1101.2397 and arXiv:1202.6330: compact-S3 field dynamics and the restrictions on time-dependent canonical scalings/shifts; these support the representation distinctions, not quantum geometry construction. https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.2397 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.6330
- Junker and Schrohe, math-ph/0109010: adiabatic/Hadamard structure and high-frequency constructions; a finite WKB truncation is not declared Hadamard here. https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0109010

These primary sources were actually opened during the continuation. No external theorem was found with all of the present required assumptions; this statement is a bounded applicability check, not an exhaustive absence claim. Mean theorems do not automatically supply complete noise, geometry-source initial correlations or an autonomous calibrated device.
