# Primary sources and their role

- V. Husain and T. Pawlowski, “Dust reference frame in quantum cosmology,”
  arXiv:1108.1147. https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1147
  Section 3.2 supplies a square-root-volume difference-ordering precedent.
  The current sign follows our declared positive-dust constraint.
- P. Singh and E. Wilson-Ewing, “Quantization Ambiguities and Bounds on
  Geometric Scalars in Anisotropic Loop Quantum Cosmology,”
  arXiv:1310.6728v2. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.6728
  Equations (2.44) and (4.9) distinguish K quantization and a spectral inverse.
- A. Ashtekar, T. Pawlowski, P. Singh and K. Vandersloot,
  “Loop quantum cosmology of k=1 FRW models,” gr-qc/0612104.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0612104
  This F-quantization model is not silently identified with our candidate.
- G. Teschl, “Jacobi Operators and Completely Integrable Nonlinear Lattices,”
  AMS Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 72.
  https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-jac/jacop.pdf
  Sections 2.5–2.6 provide spectral/Jacobi and unbounded-domain background.
- M. Radzikowski, “Micro-local approach to the Hadamard condition in quantum
  field theory on curved space-time,” CMP 179 (1996).
  https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02100096
  The finite-mode smooth-difference statement is used only on fixed classical backgrounds.
- C. J. Fewster et al., “Quantum Reference Frames, Measurement Schemes and
  the Type of Local Algebras in Quantum Field Theory,” CMP 406, 19 (2025).
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00220-024-05180-7
  Section 3.1, Example 3 gives the semibounded-clock time-POVM framework.
- B. L. Hu, A. Roura and E. Verdaguer, “Induced quantum metric fluctuations
  and the validity of semiclassical gravity,” gr-qc/0402029.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0402029
  Its intrinsic/induced and initial-state discussion informs the next gate;
  a leading large-N result is not imported as an exact two-field theorem.

The new conjunctions, exclusions, constructions, intervals and scope
decisions in this directory are project research arguments. These sources
do not certify UEOT physical unification or empirical success.

- W. Kaminski, “The volume operator in loop quantum cosmology,” arXiv:1906.07554.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.07554
  This is prior evidence for volume-domain problems in different flat scalar-clock models,
  not a theorem for our closed positive-dust family. No first-discovery claim is made.
