## What the reflection changed

A mean field equation, a normal kinematic strong constraint, a physical
Hilbert constraint and a finite-clock approximation are different claims.
The frozen mean-SCE remains valid in its stated scope. The fixed-original-
marginal normal strong constraint fails in the stated additive contract.
Physical Hilbert constructions remain possible. But neither their
existence nor self-adjoint total evolution certifies finite positive
matter stress: signed singular terms can cancel in H while K0 diverges.

Keeping the full bare W2 is stronger than keeping all shift-invariant
stress records. The new normal family is a legitimate additional source
candidate on prescribed geometries. It is not an embedding into the
rejected dust model, whose obstruction already sees the unchanged P law.
One actual chosen state, geometry and domain must be used throughout a
history; changing sigma or a boundary condition at each time is inadmissible.

The reference generator G in a code is not a scale factor. A capped
generator gives trace-norm state control; that alone does not certify
unbounded RSET, full QFT multi-time dynamics or initial normal derivatives.
The positive clock carries a finite explicit energy budget and cannot be
silently attached to the old geometry.
