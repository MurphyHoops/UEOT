# Scientific editorial changes / 科学编辑修订记录

## Edition and responsibility

This is the publication record for the complete **3.7 bilingual revision** of
*UEOT Physics*. It preserves the research content of the refreshed main edition
3.6 / UEOT-QM v31.10, including Chapter 66. The publication introduces no new
stable result ID and does not modify a physics kernel, Lean source, experiment,
or frozen Core file. The 173 inherited results include all 161 IDs present when
the bilingual work began and the 12 results independently merged in PR #450.

The initial editorial checkpoint covered Chapters 0–65 and Appendices A–F.
During delivery preparation, main advanced through PRs #450 and #451. That
advance was inspected and incorporated rather than overwritten. The initial
reviews retain their original source hashes; later incremental reviews and the
final acceptance record bind the completed 0–66 manuscripts. Edition numbers
identify publications and never establish theoretical completeness.

## Scientific interpretation

The book now presents seven distinct evidence levels: standard mathematics and
physics; conditional UEOT Core; physical-sector choices; conditional deductions;
model computations; additional testable physical hypotheses; and actual empirical
standing. These are a reader-facing interpretation of the existing dossier and
source manifest, not another scientific ledger. A result can use several levels
without proving that nature realizes its assumptions.

Standard quantum theorems retain their original scientific attribution. The
book's constructive interfaces, finite witnesses, protocol budgets and formal
bindings remain useful without turning them into claims that generic Core
uniquely selects complex quantum theory, determines every physical parameter, or
has experimentally unified physics.

## What remains OPEN

Generic Core does not by itself uniquely select complex quantum mechanics. The
physical justification of selection principles, actualization strength and an
objective-record activation rule remain separate obligations. No independent
parameter-free UEOT collapse law or newly acquired QDRR hardware data is produced
by this edition. A fitted coherence loss can still have ordinary quantum,
control, leakage, calibration or environmental explanations.

The three-generation conclusions retain their spectrum, representation,
threshold and finite-window assumptions. Wilsonian RG, Object-RG and EFT
matching are not identified by terminology alone. General continuum QFT,
nonperturbative four-dimensional construction, unbounded stopping/conditioning,
arbitrary extra memories and quantum gravity remain outside the established
finite contracts.

The new finite conditioning chapter allows a negative answer when a target is
outside the permitted action class. Its model cost advantage is conditional on
the declared resource accounting; it is not a measured device advantage.
