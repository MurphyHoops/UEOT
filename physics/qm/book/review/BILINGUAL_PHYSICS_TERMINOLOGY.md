# Bilingual terminology for UEOT Physics

Date: 2026-11-11. Initial editorial source: main `3d443ec29eb17af1fc00699a74e2d42d73516d81`; the edition-3.7 update includes terminology from the subsequently merged v31.10 research and Chapter 66. This is publication guidance, not a new scientific claim register. Stable result identifiers, existing source records, and the scientific claim ledger remain authoritative for claim identity and evidence status.

## Editorial conventions

The English text uses established theoretical-physics terminology, British vocabulary such as **flavour**, **colour**, **behaviour**, and **centre**, and **-ize/-ization** spellings such as **normalization** and **parameterization**. Use **unravelling** consistently. A research **programme** is distinguished from a computer **program**. Formula labels and executable identifiers retain their exact program spelling, and original publication titles retain their source spelling. A publisher may change regional spelling later without changing mathematical content.

Use **quantum theory** for a formal model, **quantum mechanics (QM)** for the established physical framework when appropriate, and **quantum field theory (QFT)** for a field-theoretic domain. Do not use “UEOT proves quantum physics” as shorthand for a result under an explicitly selected quantum sector. **Core** retains its proper-name capitalization.

“Certificate” means a statement of conditions and a checking procedure or receipt of the specified kind. A numerical certificate is not an experimental observation. A formal theorem retains all its physical premises after kernel checking. “Independent review” in this project means a separate internal agent or process; it does not mean external peer review.

## Formula and code policy

Stable result IDs, URLs/DOIs, numerical inputs, executable identifiers, and code blocks are retained. Display mathematics is copied exactly except explicitly logged natural-language text labels. Translating a text label such as “偶态” to “even states” does not change its algebra. Scientific corrections are applied to both language editions and recorded separately; they are never hidden as translation choices.

The complete result mapping and source manifest belong to the existing publication pipeline. This glossary does not create a second truth database.

| 半迹范数距离 / 迹距离 | trace distance, with one-half normalization | Avoid “half trace distance,” which can suggest halving the already normalized conventional distance |

The term *conditioned* refers to the stated acceptance event and the full output being compared. It does not assert a unique objective-collapse mechanism. Historical v31.10 calculations, the newly integrated research edition 3.6, and publication edition 3.7 retain separate execution identities.
