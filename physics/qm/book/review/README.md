## Exact checks and their limits

[The stable-result mapping](../PHYSICS_RESULT_MAPPING.md) is generated from both
actual manuscripts and preserves all 173 source IDs. The original 161 result
objects and the twelve subsequently merged research results remain unchanged in
the chapter dossier; this publication adds no scientific result ID.

The [display-math and structure receipt](../evidence/v3.7/bilingual-fidelity.json)
checks 726 display blocks, including eleven explicitly listed text-label
translations, and all 32 fenced blocks. The [inline-math audit](INLINE_MATH_FIDELITY.md)
checks literal Pandoc math nodes with ten individually located, reviewed
exceptions. It does not globally erase operators, variables, conditions or
punctuation to manufacture agreement. These mechanical checks supplement
independent reading; they cannot prove that prose has the same meaning.

[Source reuse](../evidence/v3.7/source-reuse.json) binds 164 proof/configuration
and runtime inputs to the executed v31.10 source, tested main and publication
baseline. The [named inventory check](../evidence/v3.7/axiom_inventory_v31_10_identity.json)
checks the exact order of 1285 source requests, including the retained 1123-name
prefix. It is not a newly executed Lean axiom audit. Historical formal and
numerical evidence is not counted as a new publication execution.

The new exact Bell-record teaching example and the statistical quantities
identified by B were checked separately. No hardware data were collected by
this publication task. The empirical report retains the ordinary-noise controls,
model assumptions, competing predictions and conditions under which a proposed
UEOT-specific mechanism would fail.
