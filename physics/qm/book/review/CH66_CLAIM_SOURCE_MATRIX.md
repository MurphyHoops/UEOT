## Attribution and unresolved obligations

The prior quantum Doob literature includes finite-time time-dependent constructions; “finite time” alone is not an originality claim. [Carollo et al., PRA 98, 010103(R) (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.98.010103) supplies that prior-art context. This chapter's exact finite-tree/support/library/resource assembly is reviewed on its own statements and code. No blanket priority claim is inferred.

The original process's success probability is not raised by running a different conditioned instrument. A realizability certificate is conditional on the specified action library and observation permissions. Device preparation, calibration, reset and recording costs need measured inputs. Numerically small eigenvalues do not justify changing support without certification. Unbounded conditioning, unrestricted coherent control and field-theoretic limits remain outside the theorem domain.
