## 6. Search limitations and scholarly attribution

The search included the book's external links, original publisher/author records, and a date-sensitive search through 2026-11-11 for Quantum Darwinism, reversibility and record criteria. Some publisher full texts were subscription-gated; some arXiv/PMC direct fetches failed while original indexed metadata or text remained accessible. Verification depth is therefore reported instead of claiming every paper was read in full. No publication priority is established merely because a formula was not found in this bounded search.

For the revised book the defensible contribution language is: explicit assumption accounting; finite conditional constructions; internally checkable countermodels; formalized interfaces; and test protocols capable of rejecting stated model classes. A genuinely new natural prediction needs an extra fixed physical premise and a difference from standard alternatives. None of the cited experiments alone supplies that premise or uniquely confirms UEOT.
