additional physical-sector assumptions, conditional deductions, model calculations,
extra testable hypotheses, and actual empirical standing. These distinctions
extend the existing dossier's status/dependency axes. They do not turn standard
quantum results into UEOT discoveries or mathematical consistency into evidence
that nature realizes a proposed mechanism.

Specific model calculations, finite formal contracts, source-bound numerical
witnesses and useful counterexamples are retained. Complex quantum selection,
actualization strength and activation, new QDRR hardware data, general continuum
and unbounded limits, and quantum gravity retain their stated OPEN boundaries.

actual protocols; they are not described as confirmations of UEOT or objective
collapse. Chapter 66's realizability and resource results remain conditional on
the declared instruments, action library, observation permissions and costs.

### Local build

Use Python 3, Pandoc, XeLaTeX and the fonts identified in the build receipts.
The Chinese layout uses Noto Serif CJK SC, Noto Sans CJK SC and DejaVu Serif.
The English layout uses Latin Modern Roman/Sans and DejaVu Sans Mono, with
CJK fallback for original source titles. `--font` can select a local CJK
font for Chinese text; it does not replace the English Latin text fonts.
**Typesetting clarification to Appendix E.2:** its generic Noto CJK default
describes the inherited Chinese template. The final English edition overrides
that default as specified above and in `header.en.tex`; the build receipts and
embedded-font inventory identify the actual fonts in each delivered PDF.
PyMuPDF and Pillow are needed for rendered-page inspection; SymPy is needed only
for the exact new teaching-example check.
